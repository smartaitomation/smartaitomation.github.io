LumaTrace Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Local health diary

Migraine episodes, symptom selections, possible triggers, relief selections, daily check-ins, notes and exact timestamps are stored locally on your device. LumaTrace does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync.

Product analytics

LumaTrace uses Mixpanel to understand whether app screens and product features work as intended. We may collect anonymous product-interaction events such as app launch, onboarding step completion, selected tab, report creation, paywall view and purchase result. We configure Mixpanel without automatic event collection or IP-based geolocation. We do not send migraine severity, symptoms, triggers, medication or relief labels, notes, diary timestamps, report contents or a health profile to Mixpanel.

Optional pattern insights

If you request a pattern insight and a remote insight service is configured, LumaTrace may send limited aggregates such as episode count, average severity, average duration and broad category counts. Raw notes, medication names and exact episode timestamps are excluded. The result is informational and is not a diagnosis. When no remote service is configured, LumaTrace uses an on-device summary.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete individual diary entries in History. Deleting the app removes its locally stored diary data from that device, subject to any device backup settings you control. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Children

LumaTrace is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to Ion Mosnoi at moshnoi2000@gmail.com. Do not include private health information in support messages.
