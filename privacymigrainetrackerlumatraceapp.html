LumaTrace Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Local health diary

Migraine episodes, warning/rising/peak/easing/clear phase checkpoints, intensity, symptom or context selections, notes and exact timestamps are stored locally on your device. LumaTrace does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync.

Product analytics

LumaTrace uses Mixpanel to understand whether app screens and product features work as intended. We may collect pseudonymous product-interaction events such as app launch, onboarding step completion, selected tab, report creation, paywall view and purchase result, together with a random app-specific identifier and basic app/device environment information. The identifier is not your Apple account, name, email address or advertising identifier. We configure Mixpanel without automatic event collection or IP-based geolocation and do not use these events to track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not send migraine severity, symptoms, context, medication or relief labels, notes, diary timestamps, report contents or a health profile to Mixpanel.

Optional OpenAI pattern insights

Nothing is sent for a pattern insight until you confirm the in-app disclosure. If you confirm, LumaTrace sends OpenAI only the selected date-range length, episode count, average severity, average duration when available, and up to three broad trigger and symptom category labels. LumaTrace does not send OpenAI raw notes, medication names, exact episode timestamps, identity, Mixpanel identifiers, exported files, App Store receipts or subscription transactions. OpenAI processes these aggregates only to return the requested non-diagnostic pattern summary. The request uses OpenAI's Responses API with application-state storage disabled. OpenAI may retain API content in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days under its API data controls unless stronger account-level controls apply. If the request is unavailable or its result cannot be validated, LumaTrace uses an on-device summary and does not consume a credit.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline every OpenAI request by cancelling its confirmation. Flight Log shows locally saved episode curves and timelines. In Vault, you can prepare a local JSON export or delete all LumaTrace data after confirmation. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored diary data from that device, subject to any device backup settings you control. It does not remove prior limited aggregates from a provider's temporary abuse-monitoring logs. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Children

LumaTrace is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
