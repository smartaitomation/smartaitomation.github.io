LumaTrace Privacy Policy

Updated: October 3, 2026

Local health diary

Migraine episodes, warning/rising/peak/easing/clear phase checkpoints, intensity, symptom or context selections, notes and exact timestamps are stored locally on your device. LumaTrace does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync.

Product analytics

LumaTrace uses Mixpanel to understand whether app screens and product features work as intended. We may collect pseudonymous product-interaction events such as app launch, onboarding step completion, selected tab, report creation, paywall view and purchase result, together with a random app-specific identifier and basic app/device environment information. The identifier is not your Apple account, name, email address or advertising identifier. We configure Mixpanel without automatic event collection or IP-based geolocation and do not use these events to track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not send migraine severity, symptoms, context, medication or relief labels, notes, diary timestamps, report contents or a health profile to Mixpanel.

Optional AI pattern insights and permission

Before each optional AI request, LumaTrace explains the data and recipients and requires your explicit permission. Cancel or dismissal sends no request. Local diary and pattern features remain available if you decline. Health summaries are personal data even when they exclude your name.

The submitted version 1.0 build 4 uses a direct OpenAI request. Later gateway-enabled builds send the request through our ShortsBlink service, operated by Ion M Smart Automation SRL, which may use OpenAI, Google Gemini or Anthropic according to availability. These providers receive the prompt and aggregate health information to produce the requested non-diagnostic pattern summary; we do not sell this information or use it for advertising.

AI data collected and purpose

The health summary contains selected date-range length, episode count, average severity, average duration where available, and up to three broad trigger and symptom categories. Raw entries, notes, medication names, exact diary timestamps, exported files and purchase receipts are excluded. Gateway-enabled builds also send an app-specific installation identifier, language, country, app/OS versions, requested provider/model, app page and development/production request context. Our service receives the connection IP address. We use this metadata for authentication, rate limits, reliability and support; it is not an Apple account, advertising identifier, email address or name.

Retention and protection

Requests use HTTPS. ShortsBlink stores encrypted request/response audit logs for up to 90 days with restricted access and automatic expiry. Deleting local diary data does not automatically remove service logs; contact us for an applicable access or deletion request. Provider safety, legal and abuse-monitoring retention is separate. OpenAI requests disable application-state storage; its API safety logs may remain for up to 30 days, subject to stated legal/safety exceptions. Other providers apply their API data terms. These are API services, not consumer chatbot accounts.

We require processors handling this information to provide protection consistent with this policy, including restricted purpose, confidentiality and appropriate security. We do not authorize provider training on your health summaries. Provider terms: OpenAI API data controls, Google Gemini API data terms, and Anthropic commercial data policy. Provider services may process information outside your country. We do not claim zero retention.

If AI service output is unavailable or fails validation, LumaTrace uses an on-device summary without consuming a remote credit. Automated summaries are informational and can be incorrect; they do not diagnose, predict medical events or recommend treatment.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline every AI request by cancelling its confirmation. Flight Log shows locally saved episode curves and timelines. In Vault, you can prepare a local JSON export or delete all LumaTrace data after confirmation. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored diary data from that device, subject to any device backup settings you control. It does not remove prior limited aggregates from gateway audit or provider safety logs. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Children

LumaTrace is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact
