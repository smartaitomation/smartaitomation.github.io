Privacy Policy

Pickleball WallCue · Effective August 10, 2026

Overview

WallCue is a solo pickleball practice planner and timer. It does not require an account and does not request microphone, camera, photo-library, contacts, or location access.

WallCue does not collect data from this app.

Practice data stored on your device

Your onboarding choices, selected language, session history, streak, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. WallCue does not upload this practice content to a WallCue account or developer-operated server.

Analytics and diagnostics

WallCue does not include an analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, advertising SDK, or developer-operated analytics service. The app does not send device or installation identifiers, app-interaction data, diagnostic data, practice audio, or practice video to the developer or an analytics provider.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions, purchase history, restoration, and offer-code redemption through StoreKit and the Apple Account. The developer receives entitlement and transaction status needed to unlock Premium but does not receive your full payment-card details.

Children

WallCue is a general-audience sports-practice tool and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. A parent or guardian should supervise practice and purchases made by a child.

Data retention and control

Local practice data remains on the device until you reset it through app controls, delete the app, or erase the device. WallCue does not retain a server-side copy of that data. You can contact us with a privacy request using the details below.

Changes

This policy may be updated when WallCue’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
