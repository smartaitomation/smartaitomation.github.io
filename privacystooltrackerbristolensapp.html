BristoLens Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Local health diary

Your bowel-movement entries, Bristol types, colours, urgency, comfort ratings, selected factors, notes and timestamps are stored locally on your device. BristoLens does not require an account, does not provide cloud sync, and does not store your photos after an AI scan finishes.

Product analytics

BristoLens uses Mixpanel to understand whether app screens and product features work as intended. We may collect anonymous product-interaction events such as app launch, onboarding step completion, selected tab, entry type logged, AI scan start/success/failure, paywall view and purchase result. We configure Mixpanel without automatic event collection or IP-based geolocation. We do not send your notes, photos, exact log timestamps, Bristol types linked to your identity, selected symptoms or factors, or any health profile to Mixpanel.

AI photo scan

The AI photo scan is optional and runs only when you tap Analyse. At that moment the chosen image is transmitted to the configured AI provider (OpenAI or Google) together with a fixed classification instruction, so the provider can return a suggested Bristol type, colour, confidence and short note. No account identifier, email address or contact details are included. Photos are not stored by BristoLens after the scan, and free scans are limited. If you do not want an image transmitted, do not use the scan and enter the entry manually.

Weekly insights

When you request a weekly insight, BristoLens sends aggregate numbers only — days logged, entry count, Bristol-type counts, an internal 0–100 consistency index, and the factor names you chose to track. Your free-text notes and exact timestamps are never sent.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The app checks your App Store entitlement locally to decide whether premium features are available.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete individual entries in the Log tab, or delete all BristoLens data from Settings after confirmation. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data from that device, subject to any device backup settings you control. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Children

BristoLens is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
