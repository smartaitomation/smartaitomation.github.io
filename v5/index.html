TWO REQUESTS. ONE UNRELIABLE REPLY.

“Where is my order?
Can I change the address?”

A polished reply invents a delivery date and claims a change that never happened.

THE ENGINEERING FIX

Identify both intents. Ask for the order number. Look up status, check the policy and confirm an action only after the tool succeeds.
