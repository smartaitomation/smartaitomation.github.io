Service

Piano Lessons: KeyBloom provides on-screen educational exercises and feedback on taps. It does not certify ability or evaluate posture, fingering or physical piano technique. No specific learning outcome is guaranteed.

Subscriptions

Starter lessons can be used without a subscription. Where offered, Premium provides the full lesson path, targeted drills and expanded progress history. The purchase screen shows Apple’s current localized price and billing period. Payment is charged to your Apple account; subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage subscriptions in Apple account settings. No trial is promised unless Apple explicitly offers it to your account.

Data and access

Progress is stored locally. You can delete it in the app. Keep your device secure. Use the app lawfully and do not redistribute app assets or content without permission.
