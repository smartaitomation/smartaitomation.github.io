TickHarbor privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026. This policy describes the current TickHarbor implementation being prepared for distribution.

Work records on your device

Job labels, work-session start and end times, break information, notes, onboarding choices and correction history are stored in the app’s private local storage. TickHarbor has no app account or work-record backend. We do not receive these records automatically.

No AI or advertising integration

The current app does not send work records to an AI service and contains no advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not request location, contacts, camera or photo access.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store subscriptions and provides the app with product information and verified entitlement status. We do not receive your card details. Apple’s own privacy terms govern its processing.

Exports and support

CSV/PDF exports are created locally. If you use the iOS share sheet, the selected destination receives the export you choose to send and its own policies apply. If you email support, we receive the message, email address and any attachments you choose to include; we use them to answer your request. Do not send unnecessary personal or workplace information.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you edit/delete them or remove the app. Deleting a job deletes its associated records after confirmation. Device backups may contain local app data under your Apple backup settings. Copies you exported are managed separately by you and the destination you chose. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion.

Security and changes

The app uses iOS private app storage and data protection. No security system can guarantee absolute protection. We will update this policy when the app’s processing changes.
