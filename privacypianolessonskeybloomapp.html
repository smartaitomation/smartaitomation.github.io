KeyBloom Privacy Policy

Updated 7 October 2026. This policy describes the current development version of Piano Lessons: KeyBloom.

Local learning data

Goal, level, onboarding state and lesson attempts are stored on your device. Attempts contain notes played, retry counts and timing measures. The app does not request microphone, camera or photo access and does not send lesson inputs to an AI service or developer backend.

Payments

Apple processes subscription payments. KeyBloom uses StoreKit to check subscription entitlements; the developer does not receive your payment card details. Subscription products are not yet available in this development version.

Deletion and retention

Delete individual attempts in Progress or choose Settings → Delete all progress. Local data remains until deleted or the app is removed; device backup behavior is controlled by your Apple settings.

Website and support

The hosting service may process ordinary request information when you open these pages. Emailing support sends the information you include to the support mailbox. Do not include sensitive personal data. Contact moshnoi2000@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Children

The app has no accounts or social features. A parent or guardian should manage purchases for children. Do not submit a child’s personal information in support messages.
