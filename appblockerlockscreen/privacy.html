Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how App Blocker: Lockscreen handles information.

Data Collection

The app keeps your tasks, Screen Time selections and lock history on your device and does not require an account. We use Mixpanel, configured for its European service, to understand app usage and diagnose subscription-loading errors.

On-Device Information

The app may store the following on your device so the app can work:

  • Selected task preferences and custom task names
  • Selected app/category labels used to describe your blocks
  • Current lock state, timers, and completion stats
  • Selected app language and subscription status shown by StoreKit

Custom task text, selected app/category labels, Screen Time selection tokens and detailed lock history are not included in our analytics events.

Usage Analytics

Mixpanel receives a random device identifier, app/version/build and device metadata, and events such as onboarding screens, paywall views, product availability, purchase outcomes and technical error codes. Events from the same installation can be linked using that identifier. We do not send your onboarding answers, custom tasks, selected apps, messages or passwords.

We disable automatic event collection and IP-based geolocation in our Mixpanel configuration. Network requests necessarily expose an IP address to the service. We do not use this data for advertising or cross-app tracking, and do not sell it.

Contact us below with privacy or deletion questions. Removing the app deletes its local data; this does not by itself erase events already sent to Mixpanel.

Screen Time Permission

The app uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks so you can select apps, categories, or websites to block. iOS handles this permission and selection UI. App Blocker: Lockscreen does not read messages, passwords, browsing content, or content inside blocked apps.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Children

The app is a productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
