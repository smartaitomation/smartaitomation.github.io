Advance the gig clearly

One stage plan. One source of truth.

PlotCue connects an editable stage layout, explicitly confirmed audio sources, a derived input list, and a technical rider in one local workspace.

PlotCue is a local-first stage plot builder for iPhone and iPad. It does not require an account, cloud workspace, or subscription.

Arrange the stage

Place performers and equipment, keep the audience edge visible, and revise the layout as the show changes.

Confirm every input

Input rows come only from microphones, DIs, and other audio sources you explicitly add. An instrument icon does not invent a channel or connector.

Review the rider

Resolve blocking issues and check warnings, channel requests, monitors, equipment, dimensions, and notes before export.

Export locally

Create a PDF rider or JSON backup locally, then choose whether and where to share it using Apple system controls.

Quick help

Why is an instrument missing from the input list?

Add the microphone, DI, line, or other audio source that will actually feed the sound system. PlotCue does not infer an input from an instrument or performer alone.

Why is export blocked?

Open Review and resolve blocking issues such as duplicate requested channels, invalid references, invalid geometry, or an unconfirmed draft assumption.

How does Smart Draft handle my description?

On eligible devices, Smart Draft uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. PlotCue does not send the description to a PlotCue server or cloud-AI provider. Quick Setup and manual building remain available.

How do I restore PlotCue Pro?

Open Settings or the PlotCue Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. The one-time purchase is associated with the Apple Account that completed it.

What if PlotCue Pro or its price is unavailable?

Keep using the free workflow and retry later. Product availability and localized price come from Apple through StoreKit.

How do I remove local PlotCue data?

In Settings, choose Delete all data and confirm. This deletes stored plots and onboarding choices from the app. Copies previously exported to Files, Mail, Messages, cloud storage, or another app must be deleted from that destination separately.
