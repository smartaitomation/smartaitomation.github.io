Privacy Policy

Teleprompter: ListingCue
Effective date: July 25, 2026

Summary

ListingCue is designed to work with public property facts. In the current release, property briefs, generated scripts, captions, shot lists, preferences, and projects are processed and stored on your device. We do not sell personal information, use advertising trackers, or collect your property content.

Property content and projects

Information you enter—such as an address or property name, public listing facts, highlights, ideal audience, and call to action—is used to create the project you requested. Do not enter client contact details, access codes, private showing instructions, or other confidential information.

Projects remain on your device unless you copy or share their contents using features you control.

Camera, microphone, and photos

Camera recording is optional. Camera and microphone access begin only after you choose to enable the camera. If you save a recording, iOS asks for permission to add it to your Photos library. ListingCue does not upload recordings to our servers.

Diagnostics and analytics

The current release records privacy-filtered feature and purchase outcome events in the device's operating-system log to support testing and troubleshooting. These events do not include property facts, scripts, captions, recordings, contact details, or advertising identifiers, and they are not transmitted to us.

Subscriptions

Apple StoreKit handles purchases, renewals, cancellations, offer-code redemption, and subscription status. We do not receive or store payment card details.

Data retention and deletion

Your projects stay on your device until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete an individual project from its menu. iOS and Apple may retain purchase records under Apple's own policies.

Children

ListingCue is a professional real-estate video tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds an external processing or analytics service, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is distributed.
