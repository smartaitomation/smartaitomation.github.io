TakePilot Support

TakePilot is a creator teleprompter being prepared for iPhone and iPad. It supports local script rehearsal and is being verified for section recording and assembled video export. An App Store release is not yet available.

Help with your projects

Edit scripts in a project. Rehearse without camera access. Recording asks for camera and microphone access. You can delete a take or project from its controls. Deleting a project removes its app-local takes; videos previously exported elsewhere remain there.

Storage and purchases

Keep enough storage for recordings and export. Original takes remain if export fails. Subscription availability and prices are shown in the app when Apple products are available. Restore purchases reconnects eligible Apple purchases.
