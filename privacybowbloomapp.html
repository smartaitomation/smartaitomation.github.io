BowBloom privacy

Draft for the current development version, 13 November 2026.

Learning data

Practice choices, settings and attempts are stored on your device. The current development version does not ask for microphone access or transmit practice notes to an AI service. You may delete practice history in Settings. Device backups are managed by your Apple settings.

Payments

Apple handles subscription payments. The app uses StoreKit to check access; the developer does not receive your payment card details.

Support

If you email support, the information you include is sent to the support mailbox. Please avoid sending sensitive information. Contact moshnoi2000@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Website

The website host may process ordinary request information when you visit these pages.
