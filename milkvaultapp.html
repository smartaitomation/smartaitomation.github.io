What MilkVault Does

  • Logs pump sessions and milk output.
  • Tracks fridge, freezer, and deep-freezer stash.
  • Shows expiry status and helps use older stored milk first.
  • Logs bottle feeds from stash and deducts used milk.
  • Exports CSV records for personal use.

Medical Notice

MilkVault is for organization and routine tracking only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, lactation treatment, or feeding recommendations. For feeding, growth, or supply concerns, contact your pediatrician or lactation consultant.
