Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This policy applies to ConsignCue, an iOS app by Smart Automation SRL.

Data Stored On Your Device

ConsignCue is a local-first app. Consignor names, contact notes, payout rates, item intake records, sale status, daily totals, drawer counts, onboarding answers, language choice, and subscription state used by the app are stored on your device.

ConsignCue does not require an account and does not upload your shop records to Smart Automation SRL servers as part of the core workflow.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Analytics And Diagnostics

ConsignCue uses Mixpanel to understand whether core screens and subscription flows work as intended. Mixpanel receives a random app-scoped identifier and limited product-interaction data such as app launch, onboarding step number, selected tab, language, app version and build, subscription product identifier, and purchase or restore outcome.

Analytics events do not include consignor names, phone numbers, notes, item titles, SKUs, categories, prices, sales amounts, drawer values, onboarding answers, photos, or other shop records.

Analytics are sent to Mixpanel's European Union endpoint. Automatic event collection and IP-based geolocation are disabled. The data is linked only to the random app-scoped identifier for analytics and is not used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Sentry crash reporting is not configured in this version. If diagnostics are enabled in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before that version is distributed.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask a privacy question or request deletion of analytics data associated with this app. ConsignCue does not maintain a user account or upload the shop records stored on your device.
