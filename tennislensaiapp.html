Main Features

Video input
Record or import a video, then select the player. Analyze up to one minute on Free or five minutes on Premium. Longer recordings open a range selector first. You can review individual detected hits afterward. Onboarding uses the same video analysis flow and includes an interactive example rally.

Swing detection
Choose forehand, backhand, serve, volley or slice. Nearby possible hits are optional previews for another analysis.

Technique report
Review preparation, footwork, contact, follow-through, and balance.

Practice advice
Get drills and concise coaching instructions for improvement.

Subscriptions

Free includes two successful video analyses per installation, each up to one minute and 20 reviewed strokes. Premium includes 10 videos per UTC ISO week, each up to five minutes and 40 reviewed strokes, and a 500-stroke weekly ceiling. Weekly limits reset Monday at 00:00 UTC. Weekly and yearly Apple auto-renewable subscriptions are available. Referral bonuses add extra videos; Premium’s weekly stroke ceiling still applies. Failed analyses do not use a video credit.
