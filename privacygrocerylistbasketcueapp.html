Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

What We Collect

BasketCue does not require an account. Grocery lists, onboarding preferences, trip history, and language settings are stored locally on your device. The app also sends limited usage events to Mixpanel, as described below.

Text, Voice, and Photos

Typed lists and photo text recognition for grocery list creation are processed on your device. Voice input uses Apple speech recognition after you grant speech and microphone permission. When on-device speech recognition is supported, the app requests it; otherwise recognition may use Apple servers. Review Apple’s speech-recognition permission notice before enabling voice input.

Subscriptions

Purchases, subscriptions, restore purchases, and offer-code redemption are handled by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Usage Analytics

Mixpanel processes limited app-use events, including app launches, selected tabs, onboarding progress, and purchase or restore outcomes. These events can include an anonymous installation identifier, app version, build, language, and event-related technical information. The app disables automatic event tracking and IP-based geolocation. Grocery-list text, photos, and audio are excluded from these analytics event fields.

Analytics events are processed under Mixpanel’s privacy practices. Contact us about privacy requests. Local lists and history remain until you delete them through the app or remove the app’s stored data.
