LumaTrace Support

LumaTrace is a local-first migraine flight recorder. It captures warning, rising, peak, easing and clear phase pulses, preserves each episode curve, and prepares deliberately shared summaries.

Contact

Please do not send symptoms, medication lists, medical records or other private health details by email.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. Use Restore purchases in Vault, or manage an active subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings.

Your local data

Vault lets you prepare a JSON export for deliberate sharing or delete all local LumaTrace records after confirmation. Saved episodes and their phase timelines appear in Flight Log.

Flight recorder

In Now, start an episode, select its current phase and intensity, add an optional quick marker, and record each meaningful change. Ending the episode saves its elapsed timeline and recovery curve locally.

Optional pattern insights

Pattern Lab asks before sending limited aggregate counts, averages and broad categories directly to OpenAI. Notes, medication names, exact timestamps, identity and StoreKit transactions are excluded. If the request fails or cannot be validated, LumaTrace shows an on-device summary without using a credit.

Medical notice

LumaTrace records personal observations. It does not diagnose, predict a medical event, provide treatment, or replace professional care. If you may be experiencing an emergency, contact local emergency services.
