LumaTrace Support

LumaTrace is a local-first migraine diary for quick episode logs, daily context, trends and care summaries.

Contact

For help with the app, subscriptions or privacy, contact Ion Mosnoi at moshnoi2000@gmail.com or +373 69 644 690.

Please do not send symptoms, medication lists, medical records or other private health details by email.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. Use Restore Purchases in Settings, or manage an active subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings.

Your local data

Settings lets you prepare a JSON export for deliberate sharing or delete all local LumaTrace records after confirmation. Individual migraine entries can also be edited or deleted from History.

Optional pattern insights

When you request a pattern insight, LumaTrace asks before sending limited aggregate counts, averages and broad categories to OpenAI. Notes, medication names, exact timestamps, identity and StoreKit transactions are excluded. If the request fails, LumaTrace shows an on-device summary without using a credit.

Medical notice

LumaTrace records personal observations. It does not diagnose, predict a medical event, provide treatment, or replace professional care. If you may be experiencing an emergency, contact local emergency services.
