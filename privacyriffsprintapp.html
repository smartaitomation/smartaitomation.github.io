Privacy Policy

Guitar Practice: RiffSprint · Effective July 28, 2026

Information Stored on Your Device

RiffSprint stores your onboarding choices, selected language, generated routines, routine-generation timestamps, completed-session history, practice minutes, streak data, journal text, and creator-pack status on your device so the app can personalize and preserve your practice flow.

No Account, Recording, or AI Upload

Version 1.0 does not require an account, record microphone audio, upload guitar performances, analyze recordings, or send your practice journal to an AI provider. Creator and teacher pack codes are checked on device and are not subscription entitlements.

Analytics

If configured, Mixpanel may receive product-usage events such as app launch, onboarding completion, routine generation, session completion, paywall view, and purchase-flow state. Events include RiffSprint as the app-name property. Journal text and entered creator-pack codes are not included in analytics events.

Subscriptions

Apple StoreKit handles purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds, subscription status, and Apple offer-code redemption. We do not receive or store your payment card number.

External Links

The app can open Apple’s App Store, email support, this Privacy Policy, and the Terms of Service. Those services operate under their own privacy practices.

Your Choices

You can edit your practice setup by restarting onboarding, clear app-local data by deleting the app, manage subscriptions in your Apple ID settings, and contact support with privacy questions.
