WordCascade Privacy Policy

Updated October 11, 2026.

This policy describes the current WordCascade app from Smart Automation SRL.

Local game data

The app keeps game boards, scores, progress, completed-level history, language and assistance preferences on your device. It has no app account or developer backend. The developer does not receive these records through the app.

Services and purchases

The current app includes no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDK or online AI service. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access. It does not perform advertising tracking.

If you use StoreKit purchases or restoration, Apple processes the request under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The app reads verified transaction entitlement information to enable Plus. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may supply store sales information through its developer services.

Support and public pages

Sending support email is optional. Your email provider delivers the information you choose to send to imosnoi@smartaitomation.com. We use it to respond to your request and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Do not send sensitive information unnecessarily.

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information according to its own privacy policy. These pages contain no developer-added analytics or advertising trackers.

Control and contact

Delete level-history records or reset game progress in Settings. Uninstalling removes app-local data; device backups are managed by your operating-system settings. For questions or deletion of support correspondence, contact imosnoi@smartaitomation.com.
