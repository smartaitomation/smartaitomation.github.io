Main Features

Room templates
Start with kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living room, laundry, and entry templates.

Recurring tasks
Daily, every-two-days, and weekly chores become a calm checklist.

Daily and weekly views
See what is due today and what is coming this week.

Quick reset
Clear fast visible tasks when the home needs a fresh pass.

Premium

CleanCue Premium unlocks unlimited custom rooms and unlimited recurring tasks. Purchases are handled by Apple StoreKit.

Languages

CleanCue supports 32 interface languages. Onboarding follows your phone’s preferred supported language. You can choose a different language later in Settings.
