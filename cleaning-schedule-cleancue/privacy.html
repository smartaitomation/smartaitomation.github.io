Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

What We Collect

CleanCue stores onboarding answers, selected language, rooms, task lists, completion history, reminder preference, and progress streak data on your device.

Local Reminders

If you enable reminders, CleanCue uses local iOS notifications to remind you about your cleaning routine. You can change notification access in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

Purchases, subscription status, restore purchases, and offer-code redemption are handled by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Accounts and Tracking

CleanCue does not require an account. We use Mixpanel in the European Union to understand app usage and improve the product. Events include onboarding steps, screen visits, cleaning-task actions, and subscription interactions and StoreKit error diagnostics, together with app version, device information, language, room count, cleaning style, and reminder preference. Events are associated with a randomly generated app identifier stored on your device. Your custom room names and task text stay on your device. IP-based location enrichment is disabled. We do not use this data for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. Contact us using the details below with privacy questions or requests.
