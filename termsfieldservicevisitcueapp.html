Subscriptions

Premium is offered as an auto-renewable weekly or yearly subscription. The live price and billing period are shown before purchase. Your Apple Account is charged upon confirmation. Unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends, the subscription renews automatically. Manage or cancel in Apple Settings. No trial is currently offered. Refunds follow Apple’s rules. Cancellation preserves access to already-saved local reports and the free workflow.

Usage allowances

Free includes three active jobs, manual reports/PDF and three successful AI drafts for the authenticated Apple app identity. Weekly Premium includes 40 successful drafts per UTC ISO week; yearly includes 80. Successful drafts count when returned, even if you decide not to save the result. Failed requests do not consume an allowance. AI needs connectivity and authentication; no unlimited AI usage is promised.
