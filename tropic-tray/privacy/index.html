PRIVACY, PLAINLY

Your tray stays on your device

Tropic Tray’s web edition has no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Gameplay works without sending a player profile to us.

The short version

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera data, advertising identifier, or gameplay analytics through this PWA.

What is stored locally

Your browser stores the current lanes, score, best score, earned coins, tutorial progress, booster counts, and sound, haptics, and reduced-motion preferences in local storage. This data stays in that browser profile. Clearing site data removes it.

Offline files

A service worker may cache the game shell, icons, and public information pages so the PWA can open with limited or no connectivity. You can remove those cached files by uninstalling the PWA or clearing this site’s browser data.

No web payments

The web shop only describes products planned for the native iOS edition. It has no checkout, takes no payment details, and does not grant paid currency or VIP status. If native purchases are later offered, Apple handles transaction data under Apple’s terms and the iOS app’s disclosures.

No cookies or third-party tracking

The current PWA does not intentionally set cookies, embed analytics SDKs, display ads, or use third-party tracking pixels. It has no backend player database.

Children and sensitive data

The game does not ask players to submit personal or sensitive information. Please do not send such information through any future support channel.

Your choices

You can disable haptics and motion effects in Settings. You can erase all locally saved progress through your browser’s site-data controls.
