TakePilot Privacy

Effective October 9, 2026. This policy applies to TakePilot.

Scripts and recordings

Your scripts, project choices, rehearsal checkpoints and recorded takes are stored in the app’s local storage on your device. TakePilot does not upload them to an AI service or developer server. No account is required. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Permissions and sharing

Camera and microphone access are requested when you choose to record. Photos add-only access is requested when you choose to save a video to Photos. Sharing through the system share sheet sends the selected video to the destination you choose; that destination’s privacy practices apply.

Apple services

StoreKit communicates with Apple for product information and purchase entitlements. Apple handles billing. TakePilot does not receive payment-card details. The native review prompt is managed by Apple. TakePilot has no external analytics or advertising SDK.

Deletion and support

Delete projects and takes using their controls, or remove the app to remove its local storage. Copies you exported, shared or backed up may remain outside the app. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include; do not send private scripts or recordings unless necessary.
