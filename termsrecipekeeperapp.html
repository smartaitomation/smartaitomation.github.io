Generated illustrations are illustrations and may not match the original dish. External-video availability and AI processing are not guaranteed. Production AI importing is not yet activated.

Import allowance

Five AI recipe imports are free in total. An active weekly or yearly subscription includes 50 imports in any rolling seven-day window. Failed imports do not consume an import. Saving Discover recipes and creating manual recipes are free. This allowance does not activate the production AI service before it is available.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. No free trial is currently offered. Apple's refund and purchase policies apply.
