ClassBrief

Create a day plan, follow each period, and hand back a useful anonymous return brief—even when school Wi-Fi is unreliable.

How ClassBrief works

1. Build the dayChoose a schedule structure and review the teacher-owned objective and materials for each period.
2. Run each periodMark a period completed, changed, or skipped and add only class-level anonymous observations.
3. Review the briefEach saved outcome appears once in the live teacher return brief.
4. Hand it backCopy or share plain text for free. Premium can add styled PDF archive and export tools.

No student records

ClassBrief is for teacher-owned plans and class-level handoff notes. Do not enter student names, contact details, grades, disability information, protected traits, accounts, photos, or behavior profiles. The app applies an on-device text check before saving or exporting suspected identifying or protected-record content.

Troubleshooting

A change did not saveKeep the draft open and choose Try saving again. ClassBrief does not report a successful save until the local snapshot can be read back.
The app says OfflineCore planning, period outcomes, the return brief, and entitled local PDF tools are designed to keep working without a network connection.
Text was blockedRemove possible identifying or protected student information, then retry. The correctable draft remains visible and is not saved or exported while blocked.
Restore PremiumOpen Settings or the paywall and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original subscription.

Subscriptions

ClassBrief includes a complete free plan-to-brief handoff. Optional weekly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions unlock unlimited saved plans and brief history, custom templates and sections, styled PDF archive/export, and local reminders. Apple shows the current price, billing period, and any eligible introductory offer before purchase.
