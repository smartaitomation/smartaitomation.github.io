Guide analytics and privacy

Smart Automation SRL offers an optional Google Analytics choice on the practical guide pages. The Google Analytics script loads only after you select “Allow analytics.” If you decline, the guide remains available and the script does not load.

What we measure

With your permission, we record page views, 50% and 90% scroll depth, section headings that enter the viewport, estimated active time at a visible section (10 and 30 seconds), table-of-contents clicks, FAQ opens, worksheet downloads, links to another guide, and clicks to an App Store page. Events include a guide slug, language, section ID and order, FAQ order, link position, app name or public App Store ID where relevant. Active-time measurement pauses when the tab is hidden or unfocused and does not prove that someone read the text. Google may also process normal browser and device data when its analytics script runs. We do not send worksheet contents, form answers, or personal app data in these event parameters.

Your choice

Your choice is saved in this browser's local storage. Use “Analytics preferences” in any guide footer to change it. Clearing browser storage removes the saved choice. Declining later stops new events from this browser session; it does not erase information already processed by Google. To ask us about privacy or deletion, use the contact information on the contact page. See Google's privacy policy for its processing details.
