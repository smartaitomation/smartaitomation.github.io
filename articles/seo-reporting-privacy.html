Portfolio SEO reporting privacy

Smart Automation SRL uses this owner-operated tool to read data for smartaitomation.com from Google Search Console and the site's Google Analytics 4 property. The requested permissions are webmasters.readonly and analytics.readonly. The tool reads sitemap and indexing status, page and event counts, and aggregated article engagement to guide editorial changes.

OAuth credentials are stored only in the owner's local, access-restricted workspace, outside the public website and source repository. The tool does not send Google account data to another service, sell it, or use it for advertising. Its reports avoid personal identifiers and use public article URLs and aggregate metrics. Google continues to process data under its own privacy policy.

The owner can revoke the tool's Google access in Google Account security settings at any time. For questions or deletion requests about locally retained reports, use the contact page. Site visitors' optional analytics choice is explained separately in the guide analytics notice.
