Portfolio SEO reporting

This is an owner-operated, local reporting tool for the practical guides on smartaitomation.com. It reads Search Console indexing and sitemap status and Google Analytics 4 article engagement so the publisher can improve guide accuracy and usefulness.

The Google authorization requests only read-only Search Console and Google Analytics permissions. It does not submit URLs, change analytics settings, edit an account, or access unrelated Google services. The tool is used only by the site owner; visitors do not sign in here.

Its local output uses article URLs, indexing status and aggregated engagement metrics. See the privacy explanation and terms.
