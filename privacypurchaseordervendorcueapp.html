Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Data Collection

Vendors, inventory items, purchase orders, receiving records, and stock counts are stored locally on your device. Optional AI import and analytics involve the data described below.

Optional AI import

You can import business, vendor, or item details from text or a photo. Text recognition from photos takes place on your device. If you choose to send the text for extraction, it is sent to ShortsBlink and its AI provider OpenAI to identify fields. The text may include personal or business information. The ShortsBlink gateway retains request and response logs for operational purposes, with a configured retention period of 90 days. OpenAI processes the text under its own data practices. Review the text and send only information you have permission to share. You can enter details manually instead.

Analytics

When configured, VendorCue uses Mixpanel to understand app usage, onboarding choices, and purchase-flow events. These events may include a device-related identifier, app version, selected options, and subscription product identifiers. Vendor, item, and purchase-order contents are not included in these analytics events.

Support messages

If you send a message using Help, your message is sent to VendorCue support through ShortsBlink and retained in its encrypted gateway logs for the configured retention period. If you choose email or WhatsApp for a reply, the address or phone number you provide is sent to support for that purpose. If you enable reply notifications, the app checks ShortsBlink for replies while open and periodically in the background, subject to iOS scheduling. An installation identifier and a securely stored access token are used to retrieve replies for your conversation. Do not include sensitive information. Chat messages are also saved on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and offer-code redemption are handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store payment card information.

Sharing

When a user exports or shares a PDF purchase order, the user chooses where to send it. The app does not automatically upload purchase orders to our servers.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning your data, contact moshnoi2000@gmail.com. You can delete locally saved records in the app or remove local app data by deleting the app.
