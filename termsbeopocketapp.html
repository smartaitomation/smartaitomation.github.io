BEO Pocket

BEO Pocket is a local banquet event order editor for iPhone. It helps organize timing, menu, setup, staffing, special instructions, and a versioned day-of PDF. It is a planning and handoff tool, not a venue-management service, booking service, staffing agency, emergency service, or guarantee that an event will be correctly staffed, supplied, timed, or delivered.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for reviewing every event name, time, guest count, menu item, dietary instruction, setup detail, staffing assignment, special instruction, PDF, recipient, and revision before you share or rely on it. Confirm current event details with the venue, caterer, client, and event team. Keep an independent record of important event information.

Local data and availability

Your BEO data and revisions are stored locally on your device. They may be unavailable or lost if the app or device storage is removed, reset, or damaged. PDF sharing depends on the iOS share destination you select. Features and Apple services may change or be unavailable.
