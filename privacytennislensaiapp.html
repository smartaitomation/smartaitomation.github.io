Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

What We Collect

TennisBlink stores onboarding choices, selected stroke, player level, camera angle, selected language, analysis reports, and premium status on your device. If crash reporting is configured, limited diagnostic and error data may be collected to improve stability.

Videos

When you choose to analyze a video, the app may send a selected three-second clip and an optional surrounding segment of up to 15 seconds, along with the selected stroke and language through our secure gateway to Google Gemini for nearby-hit detection and technique analysis after your explicit sharing confirmation. The gateway also receives an app-installation identifier, app and operating-system versions, language, and country code for service operation. The video is resized to a longest side of at most 600 pixels while preserving its aspect ratio. The ShortsBlink gateway forwards requests to Google Gemini; encrypted request/response audit records expire after 90 days. Analyzed clips and reports are saved locally for replay in History and can be deleted there. The app does not provide social sharing, public posting, or user-to-user messaging.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription status are handled by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Product Analytics

TennisBlink uses Mixpanel's European endpoint to understand product use and reliability. Mixpanel receives a persisted, randomly generated installation identifier, technical device information, app version and build, and bounded events about onboarding steps, tabs, subscription product loading, purchases, restores, review requests, and support-feedback actions. These are pseudonymous event records, not anonymous aggregate-only data. Automatic event collection and IP-based location enrichment are disabled.

Videos, coaching answers, analysis content, free-text notes, and support message bodies are not sent to Mixpanel. Apple's review sheet controls star selection; TennisBlink cannot read the stars or tell whether a review was submitted. Feedback analytics record only a fixed feature source and outcomes such as cancelled, draft saved, sent, failed, or email handoff unavailable.

Mixpanel retains analytics under the project's configured retention settings. Deleting the app removes local data but does not automatically remove analytics already sent. Contact us below with a privacy or deletion request; limited app-install context may be needed to locate a record.

Support Feedback

If you choose email support, the system mail composer or your email app handles the message you write. Sending is your choice. We receive the information you send to answer your request, and your email provider handles delivery under its own policy. Do not include private videos, passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information unless necessary for your request. Support text is not copied into product analytics.

No Tracking

TennisBlink does not use your data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Training Notice

The app provides video-analysis and training guidance. It is not medical advice, injury diagnosis, or a replacement for in-person coaching and safe practice supervision.
