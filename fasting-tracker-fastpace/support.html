Fasting Tracker: FastPace

Choose your fasting window, see its end time and keep an accurate personal record.

Your timer and records

Choose a duration you already use or have discussed with your clinician. Starting now uses the actual current time. The timer keeps counting from saved dates when the app closes. Finish and save explicitly; you can finish early. Edit or delete records in History.

Free and Premium

Free use includes timers, custom windows, seven-day summaries and editing/deleting records. Premium adds full summaries, CSV export and additional routines. Live Apple prices appear before purchase; continue free if subscriptions are unavailable. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Reminders and privacy

End reminders are optional and scheduled locally by iOS. The timer works if notifications are denied. Records remain on your device unless you choose to share an export. Deleting the app can remove local records; export before removal if needed.

Health limitations

FastPace does not recommend a fasting duration or interpret your health. Research on fasting has limitations, and this app has not been tested for health outcomes. Clinical advice is especially important with diabetes, glucose-lowering medicines, pregnancy or an eating-disorder history. Consult the source material linked in onboarding. Ending a timer is always available, regardless of payment status.
