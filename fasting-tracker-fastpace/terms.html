FastPace Terms of Use

FastPace is a user-controlled timer and personal record, not medical advice. You choose all durations and may finish at any time. It does not diagnose, treat or predict a health condition or weight change. Seek professional advice about whether fasting is appropriate for you. Basic timer use is free. Premium subscriptions, when available, are handled by Apple and renew automatically; live localized prices and billing periods appear before purchase. No introductory trial is offered in version 1.0. Manage subscriptions in Apple account settings. Restore purchases is available from every Premium page. You retain control of your records and can edit or delete them without subscribing. Apple’s standard software license terms apply to App Store distribution.

Health context

FastPace does not recommend a fasting duration or interpret your health. Research on fasting has limitations, and this app has not been tested for health outcomes. Clinical advice is especially important with diabetes, glucose-lowering medicines, pregnancy or an eating-disorder history. Consult the source material linked in onboarding. Ending a timer is always available, regardless of payment status.
