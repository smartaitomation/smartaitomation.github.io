FastPace Privacy Policy

FastPace stores your window preferences, completed sessions, optional notes, language and reminder preference locally on this device. There is no app account, analytics SDK, advertising, AI service or app server. Apple handles subscription payments. If you enable reminders, iOS schedules them locally. You control whether an exported CSV or written feedback is shared through the system share sheet. Those files include only the content you choose to share. Delete all local app data in Settings. Device backups may contain local app data according to your Apple backup settings. This policy describes version 1.0. Support and legal details are available from Settings.

Contact and scope

This policy covers FastPace 1.0. Developer: Smart Automation SRL. For privacy requests, contact moshnoi2000@gmail.com. Records remain local unless you explicitly share an export or feedback. Apple handles payment/account information under its own policies.
