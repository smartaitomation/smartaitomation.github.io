Before we work together

Practical questions.

Can you take over an existing prototype?

Yes. We start by reviewing its data flow, code, failure cases and deployment requirements. The first milestone may be an evaluation baseline or a reliability fix rather than a new feature.

Can you work with our engineers?

Yes. The engagement is designed for shared code review, clear ownership and knowledge transfer. The agreed capacity and meeting cadence are written into the proposal.

Is a workshop or AI Core required first?

No. Choose development when you need implementation capacity. If discovery shows that a workshop or a bounded foundation is the better next step, we will explain why before you commit.
