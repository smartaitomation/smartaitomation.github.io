Problems engineers recognise

Where AI projects get stuck.

RAGMore documents. Still wrong answers.

Where it breaks

Hallucinations. Poor retrieval. Outdated or conflicting documents. Duplicate content. Chunks that lose context.

What your team learns

Clean and version sources. Test chunks and search strategies. Evaluate retrieved evidence separately from the answer.

Measure: Relevant evidence found, supported answers and correct handling of unanswerable questions.

Data extractionWorks on one document. Breaks on the next.

Where it breaks

Different layouts. Missing fields. Incorrect values. Poor scans. Table values assigned to the wrong rows.

What your team learns

Test document variations. Score each field. Compare extraction approaches and route uncertain results for review.

Measure: Field accuracy, missing values and documents requiring correction.

Email answeringA fluent reply can still be wrong.

Where it breaks

Missed questions. Incomplete context. Outdated policies. Invented commitments. Staff checking every draft.

What your team learns

Evaluate real conversation patterns. Retrieve current information. Check accuracy and define when a person takes over.

Measure: Correct and complete replies, editing time and appropriate escalation.
