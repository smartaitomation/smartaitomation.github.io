Privacy &
the inquiry process.

Contacting our team

The main email button opens a draft addressed to imosnoi@smartaitomation.com. You choose whether to send it. LinkedIn and the contact form are optional external links; those services apply their own privacy policies. Opening a link does not book a workshop or subscribe you to marketing.

The project fit check

The optional quiz runs in your browser. It uses session storage to retain answers while the tab is open. It does not ask for your name, email, documents or customer data. “Start again” clears the answers. The workshop's engineer assessment is arranged separately.

Sharing your project brief

You can copy or download the brief, or open an email draft with it included. Nothing sends automatically. To open the Google contact form with answers prefilled, first acknowledge that Google receives those non-confidential answers when the form opens. Prefilled answers may appear in browser history. You still need to submit the form yourself.

The existing form uses Romanian labels: “Numele” means name, “Adresa de email” means email, “Subiect” means subject and “Mesaj” means message. The brief is in English. A submission confirmation does not prove that someone has read the inquiry.

Website data

No advertising pixel or third-party analytics script is added to the new homepage or Production AI pages. The website host may process ordinary request information. The archived website under /old/ and other existing pages retain their previous content and scripts.

Project information

We use inquiry details to respond and discuss a possible engagement. Keep credentials, personal customer records and confidential source code out of initial inquiries. Agree any necessary transfer and access process separately.

Terms and questions

Scope, capacity, dates, fees, intellectual property and support are confirmed in a written proposal. The website does not create an accepted contract.

For questions or deletion requests, contact imosnoi@smartaitomation.com. The business is Smart Automation SRL, Republic of Moldova.
