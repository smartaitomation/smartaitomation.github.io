Privacy &
the inquiry process.

Contacting our team

The email button opens a draft to imosnoi@smartaitomation.com. LinkedIn and the project quiz are optional alternatives. Opening a link does not book a workshop or subscribe you to marketing.

The project quiz

The quiz runs on ShortsBlink and adapts its questions to your answers. It asks about your project, timing, planned investment, name, email, company, position and optional comments. You can request a preferred online meeting time. Your request is only submitted after you review the answers and agree to be contacted.

Confirmation and follow-up

Smart Automation SRL uses the submitted information to assess your inquiry and follow up. Resend sends your confirmation and our team’s email notification. A summary is also sent to the team’s existing Telegram account. A meeting time is a request; we confirm availability and the meeting link by email.

Storage and your choices

Submitted quiz answers are encrypted in the server database and retained for up to 180 days, unless an ongoing engagement requires a separate record. The quiz does not load advertising pixels or subscribe you to marketing. Read the quiz privacy details for browser storage, notification services and deletion requests.

Please keep confidential documents, credentials and sensitive customer information out of the quiz. To update or request deletion of your inquiry, email imosnoi@smartaitomation.com.
