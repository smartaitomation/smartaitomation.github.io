Privacy &
the inquiry process.

Contacting our team

The Book a free call button opens a short project questionnaire where you can choose an available 30-minute call slot for us to confirm by email. Email and LinkedIn are available as text links; the email link opens a draft to imosnoi@smartaitomation.com. Opening a link does not book a workshop or subscribe you to marketing.

The project quiz

The quiz runs on ShortsBlink and adapts its questions to your answers. It asks about your project, timing, planned investment, name, email, company, position and optional comments. You can choose an available online meeting slot for us to confirm by email. Answers, including unfinished responses and contact details you enter, are saved as you go. They become a submitted inquiry only after you send the form and agree to be contacted. Unfinished drafts do not trigger emails, Telegram alerts or meeting reservations.

Confirmation and follow-up

Smart Automation SRL uses the submitted information to assess your inquiry and follow up. Resend sends your confirmation and our team’s email notification. A summary is also sent to the team’s existing Telegram account. A meeting time is a request; we confirm availability and the meeting link by email.

Storage and your choices

Drafts and submitted answers are encrypted in the server database and accessible to the authorised administrator. Unsubmitted drafts are retained for up to 30 days after the last update; submitted requests are retained for up to 180 days, unless an ongoing engagement requires a separate record. First-party reports show step views, continuations, inactive sessions, aggregate answer choices, campaign labels and coarse viewport information. No answer or contact data is shared with advertising or analytics providers. The quiz does not load advertising pixels or subscribe you to marketing. Read the quiz privacy details for browser storage, notification services and deletion requests.

Please keep confidential documents, credentials and sensitive customer information out of the quiz. To update or request deletion of your inquiry, email imosnoi@smartaitomation.com.
