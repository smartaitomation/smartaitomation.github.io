BUILD WITH EVIDENCE

Know what to fix.
Know whether it helped.

A promising demo is a starting point. Reliable AI takes a way to spot failures, measure them and make better decisions about what to change.

In this four-week course, you’ll build that process: inspect traces, learn from expert reviews, create useful datasets and test your evaluators. Then use the results to improve your agents and RAG applications.

  • Can an LLM judge catch the mistakes our domain experts see?
  • How do I reduce cost without losing the quality users need?

THE FOUR-WEEK SYLLABUS

Practice first.
Learn what the failure demands.

One connected project, from a first trace to a tested improvement. Each week combines a guided lab with an exercise you can adapt to your own use case.

01WEEK 1Build an agent you can inspectAgent foundations · tools · traces+

From a working demo to a visible system

  • Define the task, tool contracts, allowed actions and escalation rules.
  • Build a small agent loop with tool calls, stop conditions and bounded retries.
  • Log messages, retrieval, tool parameters, outputs, cost and latency.
  • Build a trace viewer that makes the failure easy to inspect.
YOUR LAB

Inspect an agent run, identify a failure and point to the evidence.

02WEEK 2Turn reviews into a test datasetHuman review · error taxonomy · synthetic data+

Useful labels start with useful notes

  • Choose a domain expert. Annotate Good/Bad with a specific reason and evidence.
  • Review across roles and conditions; group recurring mistakes into an error taxonomy.
  • Create cases from features × roles × scenarios, including clarification and refusal.
  • Check synthetic labels, preserve sources and separate train, dev and test cases.
YOUR LAB

Review a batch of traces, define your error types and build a versioned dataset.

03WEEK 3Build evaluators you can trustCode checks · LLM judges · calibration+

Test the judge, too

  • Write one pass/fail evaluator per failure mode; use code for objective checks.
  • Give a judge a clear rule, contrasting examples and an evidence-based reason.
  • Compare with expert labels: inspect false positives, false negatives, precision and recall.
  • Improve the judge on dev cases. Keep test cases untouched and report results by category.
YOUR LAB

Calibrate a judge against human reviews. Explain the disagreements instead of hiding them in an average.

04WEEK 4Improve the product. Catch regressions.Experiments · safety · monitoring+

Change one thing. Measure what moved.

  • Diagnose failures by data, retrieval, prompt, model or tool flow.
  • Try focused fixes: better chunks, query rewriting, examples, routing or smaller task scopes.
  • Compare against a baseline on the same cases, including quality, cost and latency.
  • Red-team prompt injection, unsafe tool calls and missing permissions; add human approval where needed.
  • Run evals in CI. Monitor quality, drift, cost and latency, then bring production failures back into the dataset.
YOUR LAB

Present a before/after experiment: what changed, which errors improved and what still fails.

A FEW PRACTICAL DETAILS

Before you join.

When does the course run?+

Choose 21 November–18 December 2026 (€500) or 19 December 2026–15 January 2027 (€1,000). Both run for four weeks. Exact session and office-hour times will be confirmed by email; ask Ion before paying if timing affects your attendance.

How much time should I set aside?+

Plan around 3–5 hours weekly for live sessions and independent practice. Optional office hours add 10+ hours across the course. The assignments include solutions and recorded walkthroughs.

What does lifetime access include?+

Keep access to the course recordings, materials and private Discord community. You may also join future live editions of this course without another course fee; register with Ion before each cohort. See the course terms for scope.

What if I cannot attend live?+

Course sessions are recorded, with lifetime access. Use Discord for questions and join office hours when you can. Meeting links and recordings are provided to enrolled participants.

Do I need to be an engineer?+

Engineers should be comfortable running basic Python. PMs and domain experts can focus on reviews, test cases and release decisions, pairing for coding labs. No ML research background is needed.

Who is this course not for?+

It is not a general introduction to using chatbots, a model-training research course or a promise to ship a complete product in four weeks. You should be ready to inspect examples, write review notes and test changes.

Do I need an existing product or customers?+

No. Start with the supplied materials. Learn how to build synthetic cases from roles, features and scenarios, then use expert review to check them before trusting the labels.

What will I have by the end?+

An inspectable agent, reviewed error taxonomy, versioned evaluation dataset, calibrated evaluators and a before/after improvement report. Your results will depend on your cases and implementation; the goal is a method you can reuse.

Which tools will we use?+

Python, JSON/CSV, a small agent/tool loop, code assertions and model-API calls. Use Codex or Claude to help build interfaces and scripts. The method is framework-independent; any optional model-provider usage is billed by your own provider account and is not included in tuition.

Does this cover agents, RAG and security?+

Yes. Inspect tool calls and retrieval separately from answer quality. Test permissions, prompt injection, unsafe actions and human escalation, then connect checks to CI and production monitoring.

How do I earn the certificate?+

Submit at least four of the six assignments, including the final improvement report. Ion reviews your work before issuing your named certificate. It is a completion certificate, not an accredited qualification.

What is the refund policy?+

Request a full refund before your cohort begins or within 7 days after its start. Email Ion from the purchase address. Refunds go to the original payment method; see the full course terms.

Can my company pay or enroll a team?+

Yes. Use the reimbursement email template and the payment confirmation. Book 2–20 seats together for 20% off; reply to the confirmation with participant details. Contact Ion for larger groups or private delivery.

How is this different from learning alone?+

You get a connected practice sequence, expert review, office hours, community discussion and worked solutions. The focus is making your evaluations reliable enough to guide actual engineering decisions.

Is this course affiliated with Maven or an AI provider?+

No. This is an independently developed SmartAItomation course taught by Ion Moșnoi. The listed tools and public references do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
