HOURS TRACKER · TIME CLOCK

A clear work record.

TickHarbor is being prepared for iPhone and iPad. It helps you clock in and out, separate unpaid breaks, and review your actual work sessions.

Using your clock

Create a job, clock in, start a paid or unpaid break when needed, then clock out to save. You can also add completed work using full start and end dates. Use the next date for overnight work.

Correct or delete records

Open Timesheet and select a session to edit it. Swipe a record to delete. Jobs can be renamed or deleted; deleting a job also deletes its associated work records after confirmation. An active clock must be stopped first.

Basic and Premium

Basic tracking includes one job and unlimited work records. Premium is planned to add multiple jobs, export options and correction history. Prices and availability are shown in the app when Apple products are available. No purchase is required to use basic tracking.

Subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple account used to subscribe. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
