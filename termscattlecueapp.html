Use of the App

CattleCue helps you organize cattle, breeding, due-date, calving, lineage, and herd-statistic records. You are responsible for reviewing drafts and for the accuracy and suitability of every record you save.

No Veterinary or Regulatory Advice

CattleCue is a record-keeping tool, not veterinary, medical, legal, insurance, or regulatory advice. It does not replace animal observation, qualified veterinary care, mandatory livestock records, or local identification and reporting requirements. Seek professional help for animal-health concerns and verify applicable rules in your jurisdiction.

Smart Capture

Smart Capture uses automated on-device text parsing and may misread incomplete, ambiguous, handwritten, or photographed information. Its output is always an editable draft. Review it before saving or acting on it.

Subscriptions

Premium Smart Capture limits may require an auto-renewable subscription purchased through Apple. The App Store displays the current price and billing period. Subscriptions renew unless canceled through your Apple account settings, subject to Apple's purchase terms. Restore Purchases is available in the app.

Local Data and Availability

Core records are stored locally and can be lost if the device or app data is lost. Keep appropriate backups and independent records for operational, legal, insurance, or compliance needs. App features and StoreKit availability may change, and uninterrupted service is not guaranteed.
