Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026

Information Stored in the App

Material Estimator BuildCue stores calculator inputs, saved projects, quote summaries, selected language, unit preferences, waste defaults, and AI usage counters on your device in the current version.

AI Notes, Voice, and Photos

The app can turn job notes into material estimates. With your permission, the note text, including text extracted from a selected photo, is sent through the ShortsBlink AI gateway to OpenAI for a material-list suggestion. The gateway also receives technical information including an installation identifier, app and OS version, language, and country. Requests and responses may be retained for up to 90 days. Photos remain on the device for text extraction. Voice input uses user-approved speech capture and may use Apple speech services. Do not enter sensitive personal data into job notes.

Subscriptions

Purchases, subscription status, trials, refunds, and offer codes are handled by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Analytics and Diagnostics

BuildCue sends app-use events to Mixpanel, such as onboarding, feature, subscription and purchase events, with technical context including an installation identifier, app version, language and country. The app disables automatic event tracking and IP-based geolocation. Mixpanel processes this information under its privacy policy.

Sharing

When you share a quote summary, you choose the destination through the iOS share sheet. The app does not publish your project data publicly.
