CattleCue Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026. CattleCue is designed to keep cattle records and Smart Capture content on your device.

Summary

CattleCue does not require an account, does not upload herd records to an AI provider, and does not use advertising SDKs. Animals, ear tags, breeding and calving events, notes, photos selected for recognition, and Smart Capture text are processed and stored locally.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores animal identifiers and attributes, breeding events, calving events, outcomes, notes, onboarding choices, language, entitlement state, and Smart Capture quota history locally. iOS or App Store services may retain their own purchase and device-backup records under Apple's policies.

Smart Capture and Photos

Typed notes are parsed on the device. When you choose a photo, Apple's Vision framework recognizes text on the device and CattleCue turns that text into an editable draft. CattleCue does not send the photo, recognized text, animal identifiers, or notes to a third-party AI service.

Product Analytics

When analytics is enabled in a distributed build, CattleCue uses Mixpanel to receive privacy-limited product events such as app launch, selected tab, language change, subscription state, and whether a save or Smart Capture action succeeded. Events include the app name and an app-generated anonymous identifier. They do not include ear tags, herd notes, recognized text, photos, email addresses, or raw cattle records. Mixpanel processes this data under its own privacy and retention practices.

Purchases

CattleCue uses Apple StoreKit to load subscriptions, process purchases, restore access, and read entitlement status. Apple processes payment details; CattleCue does not receive your full payment-card information.

Deletion and Contact

You can remove local CattleCue records by deleting them in the app or deleting the app. To ask a privacy question, email moshnoi2000@gmail.com.
