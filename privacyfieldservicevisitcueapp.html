VisitCue Privacy Policy

Updated October 5, 2026

Device data

Client names/contact details, sites, visits, checklist items, work notes, parts, timers, photos and reports are stored in the app’s local device storage. No account or cloud synchronization is provided. Device backups may be managed by Apple according to your device settings. You choose which photos to attach with Apple’s photo picker; the app does not request broad library access.

Optional AI and permission

Before an AI draft, VisitCue shows the selected notes and asks permission to send them to a third-party AI service. Remove names, contact details, addresses and sensitive information from these notes. Structured client/address fields and attached photos are not sent. Selected notes and chosen language pass through Smart Automation’s ShortsBlink server, hosted by DigitalOcean, to OpenAI to format a draft. If that provider is unavailable, the shared gateway may use Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude for the same request. You must review and correct the result.

AI retention

The shared ShortsBlink LLM gateway stores encrypted request and response content, including selected notes and drafts, for up to 90 days for service operation, troubleshooting and cost accounting. It also caches encrypted responses for up to 24 hours. Operational metadata is associated with an app-scoped random installation identifier. The gateway requests OpenAI responses with store=false. Providers may retain API content under their own security and abuse-monitoring policies. This is not a promise of zero retention. OpenAI API data controls explain its current practices. See also Google Gemini API terms and Anthropic API retention.

Authentication and limits

No VisitCue account or sign-in is required. The app uses its own revocable client credential for the existing ShortsBlink LLM API. AI requests include an app-scoped random installation identifier, app and operating-system version, phone language/region, screen name and build environment for service operation. Apple app/subscription transactions are not sent to this gateway. Subscription entitlement is verified with StoreKit on your device. Draft allowances are counted on this installation: three successful free drafts, or 40/80 successful drafts per UTC calendar week for the weekly/yearly plan. Failed or cancelled drafts do not consume an allowance. Reinstalling or clearing app data can reset local allowances; they are not a cross-device account balance. Contact support about gateway-log deletion; do not include passwords or keys.

Network and purchases

Our hosting infrastructure receives standard network information, including IP address and request path, for operation and security. Apple processes subscriptions, billing and refunds. VisitCue does not receive your payment card details. We do not embed advertising, cross-app tracking or analytics SDKs in this app.

Onboarding analytics

VisitCue sends selected trade and workflow choices, an app-scoped random installation identifier, app version/build, and event time to Mixpanel’s EU endpoint to understand and improve onboarding. This does not include client names, addresses, work notes, photos, or report text. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. The identifier is generated by VisitCue and is not your advertising identifier. Analytics events may queue on your device while offline and retry when connectivity returns.

Sharing and deletion

PDF sharing uses Apple’s share sheet. You choose the destination and recipient; that service’s privacy rules then apply. Delete objects in the app to remove their current local records; device backups and previously exported files may remain. Remove exported copies and manage device backups separately. Contact us for gateway-log deletion requests and include only the details needed to investigate, never passwords or keys.

Children and changes

VisitCue is a work organization tool and is not designed for children. Do not include sensitive personal, health or financial information in AI notes. We update this policy when relevant data flows change.
