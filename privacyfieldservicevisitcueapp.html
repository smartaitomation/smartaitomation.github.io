VisitCue Privacy Policy

Updated October 5, 2026

Device data

Client names/contact details, sites, visits, checklist items, work notes, parts, timers, photos and reports are stored in the app’s local device storage. No account or cloud synchronization is provided. Device backups may be managed by Apple according to your device settings. You choose which photos to attach with Apple’s photo picker; the app does not request broad library access.

Optional AI and permission

Before an AI draft, VisitCue shows the selected notes and asks permission to send them to a third-party AI service. Remove names, contact details, addresses and sensitive information from these notes. Structured client/address fields and attached photos are not sent. Selected notes and chosen language pass through Smart Automation’s ShortsBlink server, hosted by DigitalOcean, to OpenAI to format a draft. You must review and correct the result.

AI retention

The VisitCue endpoint processes notes and drafts in memory and does not store them in its database. It requests OpenAI responses with store=false. OpenAI may retain API content for abuse monitoring, normally up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its policies. This is not a promise of zero retention. OpenAI API data controls explain the provider’s current practices.

Authentication and limits

Apple-signed app and optional subscription transaction data are sent to our server to authenticate the app and verify paid allowances. We store a one-way hash of the Apple app transaction identity, a random request identifier, allowance period, status and timestamp to enforce quotas and prevent duplicate charges. These identifiers are for first-party service operation, not advertising. Free successful-draft records persist while required to enforce the lifetime allowance; contact support to request deletion. Deleting allowance records can affect AI access.

Network and purchases

Our hosting infrastructure receives standard network information, including IP address and request path, for operation and security. Apple processes subscriptions, billing and refunds. VisitCue does not receive your payment card details. We do not embed advertising, cross-app tracking or analytics SDKs in this app.

Sharing and deletion

PDF sharing uses Apple’s share sheet. You choose the destination and recipient; that service’s privacy rules then apply. Delete objects in the app to remove their current local records; device backups and previously exported files may remain. Remove exported copies and manage device backups separately. Contact us for backend-identifier requests and include only the details needed to investigate, never passwords or keys.

Children and changes

VisitCue is a work organization tool and is not designed for children. Do not include sensitive personal, health or financial information in AI notes. We update this policy when relevant data flows change.
