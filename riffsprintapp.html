Subscription Help

RiffSprint Premium is sold through Apple StoreKit. Apple handles purchase, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and Apple offer-code redemption. In the app, use Restore Purchases if you already subscribed. Creator or teacher pack codes add demo practice content and do not unlock Premium.

Free Plan

The free plan includes three routine shuffles in a rolling seven-day period, the practice timer and metronome, exercise access, and a seven-day practice journal view. A paywall may appear after the first completed session, but it can be closed to continue using the free plan.
