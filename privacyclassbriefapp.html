Privacy Policy

Substitute Teacher: ClassBrief · Effective August 25, 2026

App Store privacy summary: Data Not Collected. ClassBrief does not require an account, does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send plan or return-brief content to the developer or a third-party service.

Overview

ClassBrief is a local-first classroom-day planner and teacher handoff tool for adult educators. The current version has no developer-operated backend, no sign-in, no AI provider, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no remote crash-reporting service.

Information stored on your device

Your schedule choice, teacher-owned plan text, class-level anonymous period outcomes, return brief, templates, preferences, and locally created PDF archive files are stored in the app’s private on-device storage. This content remains there until you remove it using app controls, delete the app, or erase the device.

No student records

ClassBrief is not a student information system. It does not request student names, rosters, photos, contact details, grades, disability information, protected traits, accounts, or behavior profiles. An on-device deterministic text policy blocks suspected identifying or protected-record content before local persistence and before plain-text or PDF export. A blocked draft remains available for correction and is not transmitted.

Network and connectivity

ClassBrief uses Apple’s NWPathMonitor only to determine whether the device appears online or offline. That status check does not send your classroom content anywhere. Core planning, outcomes, brief creation, plain-text sharing, and entitled local PDF tools are designed to work offline.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, refunds, introductory-offer eligibility, and restoration through StoreKit and your Apple Account. The developer does not receive or store your complete payment-card details. The app uses verified entitlement status only to unlock Premium features.

Reminders

If you deliberately enable a local reminder, iOS may ask for notification permission and schedule the reminder on your device. ClassBrief does not operate a remote notification server.

Sharing and support communications

When you choose Share, iOS presents destinations selected by you. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies. If you email support, the developer receives the address, message, and attachments you choose to send and uses them to respond. Do not include student records or sensitive classroom information in support messages.

Children and school data

ClassBrief is intended for adult educators and is not directed to children. The app’s product design excludes student profiles and student-record storage. Schools and educators remain responsible for following their own policies and applicable education and privacy laws when choosing what to enter or share.

Security and retention

ClassBrief relies on iOS application sandboxing and local file protection. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure. Local information remains until removed as described above. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to answer the request, maintain support history, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Changes

This policy will be updated if ClassBrief’s features, providers, SDKs, or data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version.
