Privacy Policy

Substitute Teacher: ClassBrief · Effective August 26, 2026

App Store privacy summary: Data Not Collected. ClassBrief does not require an account, does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send plan or return-brief content to the developer or a third-party service.

Overview

ClassBrief is a local-first classroom-day planner and teacher handoff tool for adult educators. The current version has no developer-operated backend, no sign-in, no AI provider, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no remote crash-reporting service.

Information stored on your device

ClassBrief writes its active-plan and most-recent-return-brief state to a JSON snapshot in the app’s Application Support directory. The current Release UI does not provide an in-app local-data deletion control.

No student records

ClassBrief is not a student information system. It does not request student names, rosters, photos, contact details, grades, disability information, protected traits, accounts, or behavior profiles. An on-device deterministic text policy blocks suspected identifying or protected-record content before local persistence and plain-text sharing. A blocked draft remains available for correction and is not transmitted.

Network and connectivity

ClassBrief uses Apple’s NWPathMonitor to show whether the device appears online or offline. The planning, outcome, and brief screens do not call a developer backend. When you use Share plain text, iOS presents destinations chosen by you; a selected destination may use its own network service and privacy policy.

Purchases and Premium

The current Release build does not offer subscriptions, purchases, Restore Purchases, or entitlement-based Premium access. ClassBrief therefore does not collect or receive purchase or transaction information.

Reminders

The current Release build does not request notification permission or schedule reminders. ClassBrief does not operate a remote notification server.

Sharing and support communications

When you choose Share plain text, iOS presents destinations selected by you. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies. The public support link opens an email addressed to the support contact; anything you choose to send is handled by your mail service and the recipient’s mail service. Do not include student records or sensitive classroom information.

Children and school data

ClassBrief is intended for adult educators and is not directed to children. The app’s product design excludes student profiles and student-record storage. Schools and educators remain responsible for following their own policies and applicable education and privacy laws when choosing what to enter or share.

Storage and deletion

ClassBrief has no account or developer backend through which to request deletion of classroom content. The current Release source writes the on-device snapshot described above and does not expose an in-app action for deleting that snapshot. This policy does not make a claim about how an email provider retains a message you choose to send.

Changes

This policy will be updated if ClassBrief’s features, providers, SDKs, or data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version.
