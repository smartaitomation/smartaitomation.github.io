Privacy Policy

Updated: September 26, 2026

What We Collect

Slabeste cu Gust stores onboarding answers, selected language, meal tracking status, streak data, and an optional motivation photo on your device. If Sentry is configured, diagnostic crash and error data may be collected to improve stability.

Photos

Your selected photo and saved previews are stored on your device. Selecting a photo does not start AI processing. An optional AI preview is requested only when you choose Generate and confirm the provider-sharing notice. You may cancel that notice and continue without generating a preview.

Optional AI motivation image

When you request a motivation image and confirm the sharing notice, Slăbește cu Gust sends the photo you selected, the chosen target, the minimum profile details needed for that edit, and the device language through our secure gateway to Replicate, which runs the configured OpenAI GPT Image model. The photo and request may contain personal data. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information.

The gateway records the app, a random installation identifier, country, language, app environment, model, request input and output, status, timing, and cost for up to 90 days for feature operation, failure and abuse investigation, and spending controls. These records are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. You can cancel and keep using the app without generating an image.

Usage Analytics and Diagnostics

The app uses Mixpanel to measure product events such as onboarding progress, screen visits, product loading, purchases, and feature success or failure. Events use an anonymous installation identity and technical context such as app version, operating system, language, and environment. Our event-property filter excludes questionnaire answers, food logs, body measurements, selected photos, and raw error text. IP-based geolocation is disabled in the Mixpanel configuration.

Sentry collects crash and error diagnostics, session information, and sampled performance traces when configured. Service providers necessarily process technical connection information to receive requests. These services support app reliability and product improvement; we do not use these events for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription status are handled by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Health Notice

The app provides meal planning and tracking guidance. It is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting a diet if you have medical conditions or special nutritional needs.
