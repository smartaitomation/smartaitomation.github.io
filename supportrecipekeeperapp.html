Recipe Keeper Support

Keep recipes together, organize cookbooks and turn ingredients into a shopping list.

Import a social recipe

In the social app, tap Share, choose Share to, then Recipe Keeper. If it is not visible, tap More and add Recipe Keeper to Favorites. iOS controls the app order. Review the AI-sharing disclosure, then tap Allow & Import. Check the ingredients and instructions before saving.

Public-video availability depends on the source. Private, removed or restricted videos may be unavailable. Personal development imports are available on the configured owner device; production AI importing is not yet activated.

Organize and cook

Save Discover recipes for free. Use Save to to choose cookbooks, and Organize to set categories and tags. Open a recipe to add ingredients to Groceries. The clear shopping-list action asks before clearing items.

Subscriptions

Premium import features require an active subscription when available. Apple displays your local price before purchase. Restore purchases from Settings. Manage or cancel a subscription in Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
