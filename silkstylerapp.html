SilkStyler Support

Explore a hair change on your photo

Choose a goal, pick a clear photo, explore style directions, and compare any result you save. SilkStyler uses your phone's language and does not require an account.

Start a try-on

  1. Choose the color, cut, or style direction you want to explore.
  2. Select a clear, front-facing photo in even light.
  3. If AI try-on is available, review the photo-processing notice and choose whether to consent.
  4. Save a useful result to History to compare it later.

If AI is unavailable

SilkStyler shows an unavailable message and lets you continue browsing styles without charging a generation credit. Try again later after the service is restored.

Photos and results

Saved results remain in the app's local History. Delete an item from History when you no longer want it. A photo is sent to the AI provider only after you explicitly agree and request generation.

Subscriptions

Weekly and yearly plans use the current price shown by the App Store in SilkStyler. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID subscription settings. Use Restore Purchases in the app for an existing entitlement.

Helpful photo tips

  • Use one person, facing the camera.
  • Keep the full hairline and hair visible.
  • Choose soft, even light and avoid heavy filters.
  • Treat generated previews as visual aids; salon results can differ.

Contact

Email imosnoi@smartautomation.com and include your app version, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not email private photos unless support specifically asks for one and you are comfortable sharing it.
