Privacy Policy

Drum Workout: RudiRise · Effective September 6, 2026

Overview

RudiRise is a drum-practice utility. It does not require an account, does not record audio, and does not request microphone, camera, photo-library, contacts, or location access.

Practice data stored on your device

Your onboarding choices, selected language, completed workout history, personal-best tempos, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. RudiRise does not upload this practice content to a RudiRise account or developer-operated server.

Analytics and diagnostics

RudiRise uses Mixpanel's European service for product analytics. It sends a random installation identifier, app/version/build information, language, screen and onboarding-step activity, coarse workout progress such as tempo and completed step counts, subscription product identifiers, and purchase or restore outcomes. This information is linked to the random installation identifier so we can understand feature use and improve the app.

RudiRise disables Mixpanel automatic event collection and IP-based geolocation. It does not send onboarding answers, practice history, free-form content, payment-card details, or advertising identifiers to Mixpanel. RudiRise does not include a third-party crash-reporting or advertising SDK, and analytics are not used for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions, purchase history, restoration, and offer-code redemption through StoreKit and the Apple Account. The developer receives entitlement and transaction status needed to unlock Premium but does not receive your full payment-card details.

Children

RudiRise is a general-audience music-practice tool and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. A parent or guardian should supervise purchases made by a child.

Data retention and control

Local practice data remains on the device until you remove it through app controls, delete the app, or erase the device. Mixpanel retains analytics information according to its configured retention period. Deleting RudiRise removes local data but does not by itself delete analytics already sent to Mixpanel. You can contact us with a privacy or deletion request using the details below.

Changes

This policy may be updated when RudiRise's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
