Privacy Policy

Drum Workout: RudiRise · Effective July 28, 2026

Overview

RudiRise is a drum-practice utility. It does not require an account, does not record audio, and does not request microphone, camera, photo-library, contacts, or location access.

Practice data stored on your device

Your onboarding choices, selected language, completed workout history, personal-best tempos, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. RudiRise does not upload this practice content to a RudiRise account or developer-operated server.

Analytics and diagnostics

When analytics are enabled in the distributed build, RudiRise may use Mixpanel for product-interaction events. Mixpanel may process device or installation identifiers and app-interaction information so the app can be improved. RudiRise does not include a third-party crash-reporting or advertising SDK. Practice audio is never collected, and the information is not used for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions, purchase history, restoration, and offer-code redemption through StoreKit and the Apple Account. The developer receives entitlement and transaction status needed to unlock Premium but does not receive your full payment-card details.

Children

RudiRise is a general-audience music-practice tool and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. A parent or guardian should supervise purchases made by a child.

Data retention and control

Local practice data remains on the device until you remove it through app controls, delete the app, or erase the device. Analytics and diagnostic providers retain information according to their own policies and configured retention periods. You can contact us with a privacy request using the details below.

Changes

This policy may be updated when RudiRise's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
