Quick help

Why is a field missing?
Some identifiers do not include author, year, publisher, or edition metadata. Review the warning before you export.
Where are citations saved?
Your saved citations stay on your device unless you choose to share an export.
Camera unavailable?
Use the manual barcode field. The Simulator and some devices may not provide a barcode camera.

Subscriptions

CiteSnap Plus is managed by Apple through your Apple ID. Use Restore Purchases in the app if you already subscribed. You can manage or cancel a subscription in App Store account settings.
