Guitar Practice: RiffSprint · Effective July 28, 2026

Use of the App

RiffSprint helps organize guitar-practice routines, exercises, timing, tempo, history, and journal notes. Generated routines are practice suggestions. You remain responsible for choosing safe tempos, technique, duration, equipment, and volume.

Free and Premium Access

The free plan includes three routine shuffles in a rolling seven-day period plus the available free timer, metronome, exercise, and recent-journal features. Premium can unlock unlimited routine generation, longer history, analytics, custom sessions, saved routines, and saved packs. Exact features may evolve while preserving any entitlement required by Apple’s rules.

Subscriptions and Apple Offer Codes

Premium may be sold through weekly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. Apple handles billing, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and offer-code redemption under your Apple ID settings and App Store terms. Subscription prices are displayed by the App Store before purchase.

Creator and Teacher Pack Codes

Creator or teacher pack codes may add demonstration routines or exercises. They do not provide paid subscription access, discounts, or external purchasing. Apple offer codes must be redeemed through Apple’s official redemption sheet.

Health and Hearing

Stop practicing if you experience pain, numbness, strain, or hearing discomfort. Use reasonable volume and breaks. RiffSprint is not medical advice or a substitute for a qualified music teacher, hearing professional, or clinician.

Acceptable Use
