Recommendation

Add Learn alongside the current app.

Keep the 40-rudiment library and hands-free tempo ladder. Add an optional guided route that explains what to practice, what to listen for, and how to use the pattern in music.

Who benefits most?

A beginner who has learned basic strokes through a few lessons or self-teaching, but asks: “What should I practice next?” Prior classes are not required.

Complete beginners get a short starting guide; proper grip, posture and stroke demonstrations still need to be created. Experienced players can skip lessons and go straight to practice.

Start small

Release three lessons: single strokes, double strokes and paradiddles. Each should include a visual/audio demonstration, three focused exercises, a musical application and a saved reflection.

Why this could be better

The current app helps users increase practice tempo. Learn adds purpose and structure: understand → practice deliberately → apply musically → choose the next session.

This is a product recommendation, not a proven improvement. Test whether early-stage drummers can choose an exercise, explain its purpose and return to practice with less confusion.
Design notes

What the user needs first

A pair of sticks, a practice pad or suitable quiet surface, and a basic understanding of grip and relaxed strokes. A drum kit and music-reading skills are optional for the first lessons.

R/L and subdivisions are explained inside the lesson. Grip, posture and pain cannot be assessed by this app; practical instruction from a teacher remains useful.

Keep progress honest

“Comfortable BPM” is reported by the drummer. Completing a lesson does not mean the app verified technique, timing or mastery. No microphone scoring is proposed for the first version.

A focused first release

Single strokes, double strokes and paradiddles. Each gets original explanations, animated sticking, three exercises and a musical application.

Proposed free value: starting guide and the full single-stroke lesson. Premium: additional lesson paths and custom practice, subject to a later pricing decision. Existing entitlements should carry through.

Inspired by the learning structure of Drumeo’s guide. All proposed lessons, notation and audio must be original or licensed. No PDF pages or recordings are embedded.
