Website privacy

Updated October 3, 2026. This notice covers the RudiRise download/guide page on smartaitomation.com. The iOS app has a separate privacy policy.

Optional advertising measurement

We use our shared Meta Pixel, also used by GenBlink and ShortsBlink, only after you allow advertising cookies. Meta may receive page views, clicks on the App Store or free-guide links, and automatic App Store handoffs, plus browser/device information, your IP address, page URL and advertising cookie identifiers. We send the fixed app/offer name and the ad creative/placement label with our custom events. Automatic handoffs are recorded separately from intentional clicks. We do not ask for an email address, use advanced matching, or send your drumming records from this page.

Meta uses this information for advertising measurement and its services under Meta’s Privacy Policy. Your browser may block the Pixel even after consent. A website click or automatic handoff does not prove an app install or purchase.

Your choice and retention

Decline is available alongside Allow. Downloads and the App Store redirect work without optional tracking. Use Cookie settings at the bottom of the download page to change your choice. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. When allowed, Meta may set _fbp and _fbc advertising cookies; the click identifier stored by this page expires after 90 days. Declining stops our new Pixel events and removes those cookies for this website where browser access permits. Already transmitted data cannot be recalled by clearing a cookie.

Hosting and external links

The page and free PDF are hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives normal web requests and may process IP addresses and technical logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. The app-offer page automatically opens Apple’s website or App Store app after about two seconds; Stay on this page cancels it. PDF-offer visits do not automatically redirect. Clicking App Store also opens Apple; Apple’s privacy practices then apply. We forward no raw Meta click identifier or personal information to the App Store. If an Apple campaign link is configured, its public provider/campaign labels enable Apple’s aggregate campaign reporting.
