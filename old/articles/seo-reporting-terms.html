Portfolio SEO reporting terms

This owner-operated reporting tool is provided by Smart Automation SRL to review its own public site's Search Console and Google Analytics data. It is not a public service or a visitor login. Authorized use is limited to the site's owner and people the owner expressly permits to operate it.

The tool reads data through Google's APIs using read-only permissions. It does not guarantee that a page will be indexed, ranked, visited or downloaded. Google can change API availability, quotas and reported data. Decisions based on reports remain the publisher's responsibility.

Access may be stopped by revoking the Google authorization. Questions about this tool can be sent through the contact page.
