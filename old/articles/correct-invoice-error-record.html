A draft typo and an issued tax document are different problems

For an unsent draft, correct the actual source details and recheck the preview before issuing it. For an issued invoice, retain the version the customer received and its number/date. A correction needs a traceable relationship to that original, rather than two unrelated invoices that both appear payable.

Ask whether payment has been received, whether the customer recorded the invoice, and whether tax was reported. These facts can change the proper process. A label such as Void in an app does not by itself reverse a payment, notify a customer or correct a tax return.

As a specific UK VAT example, GOV.UK requires retention of issued/received invoices and credit/debit notes, while Notice 700/45 describes correcting VAT errors and distinguishes customer-document corrections from tax-account/return adjustments. Do not apply that framework automatically to another country or a non-VAT transaction. Use current local guidance or an accountant for the actual method.

Worked trail: a duplicated service line

Fictional invoice INV-104 was issued with a duplicated USD 40 service line. The original total is USD 240; the checked service total should be USD 200 before any jurisdiction-specific tax treatment. This is arithmetic, not a prescribed credit-note procedure.

Do not issue another USD 200 demand and leave the USD 240 invoice apparently open without explaining their relationship. Equally, do not call USD 40 refunded until an actual refund is confirmed. A proposed adjustment, agreed balance and completed payment are separate states.

The worksheet leaves the correction-document type blank until the applicable process is known. It does not supply an invented legal number sequence. Keep a dated explanation of what changed and why, and preserve customer-facing communication through your normal business process.

Recheck more than the final total

Compare client identity, invoice number, dates, description, quantity, rate, discount, tax treatment, currency and payment status. A copied line can change more than one amount. If tax was included or already reported, avoid treating the pre-tax arithmetic above as the finished corrected balance.

Before sending any replacement or adjustment, verify which document supersedes or adjusts which original, what the customer should pay now, and whether another accounting action is still outstanding. Keep an internal note distinct from the document sent to the customer.

Do not put bank account details or private client information into a public correction example. Store the actual documents securely and share them only with the intended customer or adviser through an appropriate channel. This guide drafts a record structure; it sends nothing on your behalf.

Questions answered

Can I simply overwrite an invoice already sent?

Preserve what was issued and check the proper correction process. An edited local record alone does not explain the original customer document.

Does marking an invoice Void refund it?

No payment or refund is established by a status label. Record and verify the actual money movement separately.

Does this guide tell me which credit note to issue?

No. Document and tax-correction requirements depend on jurisdiction, transaction and reporting state.
