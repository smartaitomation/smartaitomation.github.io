The fit check

The quiz runs in your browser. It uses session storage to keep your selected answers while this tab is open. It does not ask for your name, email, confidential documents or customer data. You can clear the answers using “Start again.”

No advertising pixel or third-party analytics script is added to these Production AI pages. The website host may process ordinary request information to serve and protect the site.

Sharing your brief

At the result, you can copy or download the brief. If you choose “Continue to contact form,” the selected non-confidential answers are passed to our existing Google Form in a prefilled link. Google receives those answers when the form opens; they may also appear in browser history. The quiz clearly asks you to acknowledge this before opening it.

The form currently uses Romanian field labels: name, email address, subject and message. Your English project brief is prefilled. Add a way to contact you and submit there. Opening the form or generating a brief does not send an inquiry. The form’s confirmation screen indicates that Google accepted the submission; it does not prove that someone has read it.

You may instead use the email link. It opens a draft in your email application. You must send the message yourself. We use inquiry details to respond to your request and discuss a possible engagement. Completing this quiz does not subscribe you to a marketing sequence or commit you to a purchase.

Keep sensitive information out

Do not include API keys, passwords, personal customer records, confidential source code or regulated data. If a project needs sensitive material, we will agree an appropriate transfer and access process separately.
