Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Information Stored in the App

CraftCue stores craft inventory items, project plans, wishlist entries, onboarding answers, selected language, subscription status, and Quick Add usage counters on your device in the current version.

Quick Add

The original text Quick Add uses local parsing on your device. Optional Quick AI and Fill with AI are different: after you accept the in-app notice, a typed or recorded request and relevant item context are sent through the ShortsBlink gateway to OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or DeepSeek to prepare editable supply, project, or wishlist fields. Recorded audio is sent to OpenAI GPT Transcribe through the gateway first. You review and correct the draft before saving. Do not include sensitive personal, financial, health, or account information in AI requests.

The gateway may retain request text, transcripts, responses, and technical usage records for up to 90 days for operation, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and spending controls. You can use manual entry and local Quick Add without sending an AI request.

Subscriptions and Offer Codes

Purchases, subscription status, free trials, refunds, renewals, cancellations, and offer-code redemption are handled by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Analytics and Diagnostics

CraftCue uses Mixpanel to understand app usage and improve the product. Events include onboarding progress, tab visits, inventory and Quick Add actions, and subscription interactions, together with app version, language, technical device information, product identifiers, and StoreKit error codes. Events are associated with the SDK’s anonymous installation identifier. Inventory notes, project and wishlist text, and onboarding answers are not included in these analytics events. Automatic event tracking and IP-based location enrichment are disabled.

CraftCue uses Sentry for crash, performance, session, and technical error diagnostics. Diagnostic events include a persistent installation identifier so reports from the same installation can be associated. The app does not attach your inventory, project, or wishlist content to diagnostic reports. CraftCue does not use these services for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

Device Permissions

Core inventory, project, wishlist, and settings features do not require camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, precise location, or health data access. The optional voice Quick AI action asks for microphone permission when you start recording.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete local app data by removing the app from your device. You can manage subscriptions through your Apple ID App Store subscription settings.
