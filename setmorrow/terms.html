General fitness planning

SetMorrow offers planning tools, illustrative exercise guides, and on-device camera estimates for rep timing and form. Camera cues are approximate and can miss or misclassify a rep. It is not a medical device, diagnostic service or substitute for individualized professional guidance. Exercise within a comfortable range and stop if you feel pain. Consult a qualified professional when health conditions or injuries affect safe exercise.

Optional AI features

SetMorrow 1.0.2 can use AI to read a cardio machine display photo with your permission. Review and correct the suggested values before logging them. Network, App Store verification and provider availability can affect AI access. On-device reading and manual entry are available.

SetMorrow 1.0 offered optional AI plan adjustments. Review any suggestion before exercising. A suggested plan does not establish medical suitability.

Subscriptions

Free features and Plus benefits are described in the app. Apple shows the current localized subscription price and duration before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel through App Store account settings. Restore purchases is available in the app.

Version 1.0 included allowances for optional AI adjustments. Version 1.0.1 describes its current Plus benefits in the app.

In SetMorrow 1.0.2, AI photo requests are limited to five per rolling 24 hours per verified App Store account. Free accounts can make up to six requests in a rolling seven-day period and 20 free requests in total. Each request accepted by the gateway counts toward the limits, including a provider failure after acceptance. Apple shows the terms of any subscription offer before redemption.

Your records

You control local records and exports. Maintain your own backups if needed. No cloud account or synchronization service is provided.
