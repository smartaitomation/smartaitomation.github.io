SETMORROW

Build better workout form

SetMorrow helps you plan workouts, practice controlled reps, and track your progress at home or in the gym.

Your next useful session

Review movement illustrations, swap compatible exercises, record sets and effort, and see progress from workouts you actually saved. Your completed sets remain in your history when you shorten a session.

Workout camera in 1.0.1

Open Today, choose Start live workout or Analyze video, and pick a supported lift. For live feedback, position your iPhone so your whole body is visible from the side. Listen for rep counts and form or pace cues. Open History to replay your recordings and individual reps. The Learn tab and exercise guides explain setup and movement.

Purchases and restoration

Current subscription prices and renewal periods appear in the app before purchase. Open Settings → Restore purchases to recover an active Apple subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account.

Local data

No SetMorrow account is required. Plans and history are stored on your device. Settings lets you export your workout plan and log data or clear local app data. On-device workout camera recordings and rep history remain in the app until you clear them. Device backups are controlled by your operating-system settings.

Contact support

In SetMorrow 1.0.1, open the Help bubble or Settings → Help to write to support and check replies. You can also email moshnoi2000@gmail.com with your app version and a description of the issue. Keep health records and payment credentials out of support messages.

SetMorrow supports general fitness planning. It does not diagnose injuries, provide rehabilitation or guarantee physical outcomes.
