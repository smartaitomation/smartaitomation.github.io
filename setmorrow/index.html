SETMORROW

A workout that fits today.

SetMorrow is a local workout planner for home and gym. Choose your time and equipment, create a realistic week, and adjust the remaining work when your day changes.

Your next useful session

Review movement illustrations, swap compatible exercises, record sets and effort, and see progress from workouts you actually saved. Your completed sets remain in your history when you shorten a session.

Help with your plan

Open Plan → Edit preferences to change your routine. Use Today → Fit today for a one-session adjustment. Exercise guides include play, pause and static start/end poses.

Purchases and restoration

Current subscription prices and renewal periods appear in the app before purchase. Open Settings → Restore purchases to recover an active Apple subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account.

Local data

No SetMorrow account is required. Plans and history are stored on your device. Settings lets you export your data or delete it. Device backups are controlled by your operating-system settings.
