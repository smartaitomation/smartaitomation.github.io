SETMORROW

Privacy policy

Effective September 19, 2026. Provided by Ion M Smart Automation SRL.

What stays on your device

Your preferences, plans, exercise favorites, completed sets, loads, dates and effort ratings are stored locally. There is no SetMorrow account or app cloud-sync service. We do not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Optional AI adjustments

AI requests require your in-app consent. An adjustment sends your chosen time budget, general fitness goal, experience category, effort choice and compatible exercise catalog to OpenAI. Names, photos, body measurements, raw workout history, precise workout timestamps and analytics identifiers are not part of that request. You can review the suggestion before changing your plan. Manual planning remains available when AI is unavailable or declined.

Requests disable response application-state storage. Provider abuse-monitoring retention may still apply according to OpenAI’s API data controls and privacy policy. Local deletion does not remove information already processed under the provider’s retention rules.

Optional product analytics

Sharing basic app usage is off by default. If enabled, Mixpanel receives a pseudonymous installation identifier, basic app/device context and selected navigation or subscription-loading events. Automatic events, session replay, advertising identifiers and IP-based geolocation are disabled. We do not send goals, equipment, exercise names, workout records, body information or AI inputs/results to Mixpanel. You can turn this off in Settings.

Apple purchases

Apple handles subscription transactions. We use StoreKit to check entitlements and do not receive your payment-card information. App Store transactions follow Apple’s privacy policies.

Your choices

Settings provides local JSON export, deletion of local records and consent controls. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to backups managed by you. Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions separately in your Apple Account.
