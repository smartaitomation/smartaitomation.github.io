SETMORROW

Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026. Provided by Ion M Smart Automation SRL.

What stays on your device

Your preferences, plans, exercise favorites, completed sets, loads, dates and effort ratings are stored locally. There is no SetMorrow account or app cloud-sync service. We do not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Optional AI adjustments

AI requests require your in-app consent. An adjustment sends your chosen time budget, general fitness goal, experience category, effort choice and compatible exercise catalog through ShortsBlink to OpenAI. The request also carries a pseudonymous app installation identifier, app and operating-system versions, language, country, and receipt environment so ShortsBlink can authorize and process it. The country is derived from device locale settings; the app does not request precise location. ShortsBlink retains encrypted request/response audit records for up to 90 days and records a keyed hash of the request’s network IP address for abuse monitoring. Names, photos, body measurements, raw workout history, precise workout timestamps and Mixpanel analytics identifiers are not part of the AI request. You can review the suggestion before changing your plan. Manual planning remains available when AI is unavailable or declined.

Requests disable response application-state storage. Provider abuse-monitoring retention may still apply according to OpenAI’s API data controls and privacy policy. Local deletion does not remove information already processed under the provider’s retention rules.

Product analytics

Basic app-usage analytics are disabled in the current app. Earlier opt-in settings are switched off on launch. SetMorrow does not send navigation, workout data or AI inputs/results to Mixpanel while analytics are disabled.

Written feedback

If you choose Send feedback from Settings or the feedback form after a rating selection, your message, selected app-feedback star rating (when applicable), form context, and a pseudonymous installation identifier are sent to ShortsBlink for storage in an encrypted support log. The support log also records a keyed hash of the request’s network IP address for abuse monitoring. Usage analytics are disabled; feedback is not sent to Mixpanel. The feedback form does not send a star rating to Apple. Avoid including health details or other sensitive information. Selecting five stars in the app requests Apple’s native review prompt after the app modal closes. Apple controls whether that prompt appears; selecting stars in SetMorrow does not submit an App Store rating. Written feedback is sent only when you tap Send feedback.

Apple purchases

Apple handles subscription transactions. We use StoreKit to check entitlements and do not receive your payment-card information. App Store transactions follow Apple’s privacy policies.

Your choices

Settings provides local JSON export and deletion of local records. The AI adjustment screen shows a compact disclosure naming ShortsBlink and OpenAI, a link to this policy and an explicit permission switch before any AI request. You can withdraw AI permission on that screen; declining permission leaves manual planning available. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to backups managed by you. Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions separately in your Apple Account.
