SETMORROW

Privacy policy

Updated November 24, 2026. Provided by Ion M Smart Automation SRL.

What stays on your device

Your exercise preferences, plans, favorites, sets, loads, dates, and effort ratings are stored locally. SetMorrow has no app account or app cloud sync. Device backup settings can apply to local files.

Workout camera and video history

SetMorrow 1.0.1 asks for camera access when you use the live workout camera. It does not record microphone audio. When you choose Analyze video, the system picker gives SetMorrow access to the video you select. Apple Vision estimates body positions on your device to count reps and generate form or pace cues. The app stores saved recordings or selected video copies and their rep timing and form results locally for replay. SetMorrow does not send your workout recordings, pose estimates, or rep history to the support service. You can manage camera access in iOS Settings.

Support chat and written feedback

When you tap Send in Help or submit written feedback, your text is sent over HTTPS to ShortsBlink for the SetMorrow support team. Help uses a random conversation identifier so replies reach the same installation without an account. If you select Reply here, the support team can see that preference. The service stores message content in encrypted support records and records app and operating-system versions, app language, and a keyed hash of the network IP address for security and abuse prevention. Support messages are retained under the service’s 90-day log policy. The app also keeps a local copy of the thread so you can read it later. Replies are checked while Help is open. Please keep health details, credentials, and payment information out of messages.

Founder links open external services. Each service handles any information you choose to send under its own policies.

Optional AI adjustments in earlier versions

SetMorrow 1.0 offered optional AI adjustments. With explicit consent, an adjustment sent a time budget, general fitness goal, experience category, effort choice and compatible exercise catalog through ShortsBlink to OpenAI. ShortsBlink also received pseudonymous app and technical details to authorize and process the request. Encrypted gateway records were retained under the gateway log policy. OpenAI’s API abuse-monitoring retention may apply under its data controls. SetMorrow 1.0.1 uses on-device camera estimates for its form coaching and has removed the AI adjustment feature.

Product analytics

SetMorrow disables product analytics. The app does not use support data or workout recordings for tracking or advertising.

Apple purchases

Apple handles purchase and subscription transactions. SetMorrow uses StoreKit to check entitlements. Apple’s privacy policies apply to its transactions. SetMorrow does not receive your payment-card information.

Your choices

You can export plans and logged workouts as JSON in Settings. In version 1.0.1, Delete all local data also clears in-app recordings and the local support conversation. Original videos in Photos and previously sent support messages remain subject to their own storage and retention settings. Removing the app clears its local container, subject to backups you control. You can contact us about your support data. Manage subscriptions separately in your Apple Account.
