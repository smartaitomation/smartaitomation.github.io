BristoLens Support

BristoLens is a local-first bowel-movement diary for quick Bristol-type logs, daily context, trends and an optional AI photo scan.

Please do not send symptoms, medical records, photos or other private health details by email.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. Use Restore Purchases in Settings, or manage an active subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings. Manual logging, history and weekly insights remain free; the subscription increases the AI photo-scan allowance from 3 free scans to up to 200 scans.

Your local data

Everything you log stays on this phone. BristoLens has no account and no cloud sync. Settings lets you delete all local data after confirmation, and individual entries can be edited or deleted from the Log tab.

AI photo scan

When you tap Analyse, the photo you chose is sent to the configured AI provider so it can suggest a Bristol type and notes. Free scans are limited; the app shows how many remain. AI suggestions can be wrong and you can always set the type yourself.

Medical notice

BristoLens records personal observations. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for professional care. Contact a healthcare professional about persistent or worrying changes, and contact local emergency services if you may be experiencing an emergency.
