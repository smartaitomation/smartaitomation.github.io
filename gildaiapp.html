GILD AI

Makeup inspiration, matched to your routine.

What the app does

Choose your skin type, experience level and goal. GILD AI matches those answers to a curated makeup look, then gives you a clear tutorial and saves a starter path on your device.

The current catalogue includes everyday, event and creative looks. You can browse the look image, description and ordered steps without creating an account.

Photo preview

Photo preview is optional. Before a photo is sent for AI processing, GILD AI names the processing purpose and asks for your consent. You can decline and continue with the manual tutorial.

If the secure AI service is unavailable, the app reports that clearly and keeps the selected look’s manual steps available. Generated previews can vary and are not a promise of a real-world result.

Premium and purchases

GILD AI offers optional weekly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions. The app loads the current localized price and available offer directly from the App Store. A free path remains available after onboarding.

To restore an existing purchase, open the Premium screen and choose Restore Purchases. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. GILD AI stores onboarding choices and routine preferences locally on the device.

Why can’t I use the camera?

Camera capture requires camera permission and an available device camera. You can also choose an image from your photo library.

How do I withdraw photo-processing consent?

Open Profile and use the AI data-sharing consent control. This prevents future photo uploads until you consent again.

How do I reset onboarding?

Open Profile and choose the onboarding reset option.
