Common Questions

Does CattleCue work without internet?
Yes. Animals, breeding events, calvings, notes, due dates, and statistics are stored and calculated on your device.

How does Smart Capture work?
Typed field notes are parsed on the device. Photo capture uses Apple's on-device Vision text recognition, then creates an editable draft. Review every draft before saving it.

Where are my records stored?
Your herd records remain on your device. Deleting the app can remove local data, so keep any records you need through your normal device backup practices.

Why are subscription prices unavailable?
Prices are loaded from the App Store. Check your connection and App Store account, then try again or use Restore Purchases.
