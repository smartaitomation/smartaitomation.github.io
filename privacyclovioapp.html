Clovio Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 21, 2026.

Clovio helps users create a closet database, outfit matches, shop-match feedback, body profile estimates, and try-on previews.

Data You Provide

You may choose to add clothing photos, shop item photos, body profile photos, body profile values, clothing notes, outfit prompts, and shopping prompts. You provide this content when you enter text in the app or choose a camera, photo-library, or upload button.

Storage

The app is designed to store closet items, clothing attributes, usage counters, body profile values, selected photos, generated previews, and your AI-processing consent choice on the device.

AI Processing

Before Clovio sends personal data to a third-party AI service, the app displays an in-app consent screen that explains the transfer and asks for your permission. If you do not allow AI processing, local closet and outfit features remain available.

When you allow AI processing and request AI closet detection or AI try-on previews, Clovio sends the selected photos, cropped clothing images, body profile values, closet item details, or text prompts needed for that request through the ShortsBlink AI gateway to Replicate, Inc. and its model providers to return the requested analysis or generated image. The gateway also receives technical request information, including an installation identifier, app version, device OS version, and language.

Third-party services process these requests under their own applicable privacy terms.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple StoreKit. Clovio does not receive your full payment card details.

Analytics

Clovio sends limited app-use events to Mixpanel, including app launches, onboarding progress, selected tabs, and purchase or restore outcomes. Events may include an installation identifier, app version, language, and technical information related to the event. The app disables automatic event tracking and IP-based geolocation. Photos and free-form clothing notes are not included in these analytics event fields. Mixpanel processes this information under its privacy policy.
