THE COMMON TRAPS

More tutorials do not automatically mean more progress.

Learning tools without a reason

It is easy to collect frameworks without understanding what each one solves. Start with the concept and the decision it supports.

Trusting the first good-looking answer

A convincing response can still be wrong. Learn to inspect examples, define expected behavior and check the result.

Skipping feedback and comparison

Without a clear starting point and a way to check changes, it is hard to know what to improve next.

QUESTIONS YOU MAY HAVE

Let’s make the next step clear.

Do I need AI experience already?

No. Choose the beginner path if AI applications are new to you. You can leave any concepts unchecked and change your answers.

What if I already built a demo or deployed an agent?

Choose the path that matches your experience. The concept checklist changes so you can identify a more relevant starting point.

How much time do I need?

Six weeks is a proposed framework, not a fixed promise about hours or outcomes. Adapt your pace to your experience and available time.

Will this guarantee a higher salary or AI job?

No. Career and income outcomes depend on your experience, market, opportunities and effort. The roadmap helps you plan your learning.

Is mentorship included?

The roadmap is a free resource. The optional call is a chance to discuss whether mentoring fits; any paid scope and terms would be discussed separately.

Why ask about income?

Optional income ranges help describe your starting point and goal. You can choose not to answer; no payslip or exact salary is needed.
