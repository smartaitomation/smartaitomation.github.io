Drum Workout: RudiRise

Build clean speed with automatic BPM steps, all 40 PAS rudiments, and personal-best history.

How a workout works

1. Choose
Pick a rudiment or use today's personalized ladder.
2. Play
Follow the sticking and audible metronome while BPM advances automatically.
3. Decide
Pass, repeat, or back off by 2 BPM whenever technique needs it.
4. Progress
Completed ladders save session history and your clean personal best.

Metronome or sound issue

Check that your device volume is audible and Silent Mode or audio routing is not suppressing playback. Pause and resume the workout to restart the click engine.

Restore Premium

Open Settings in RudiRise and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. Offer codes can be redeemed from the same screen through Apple's redemption sheet.

Subscriptions and free access

RudiRise includes eight free foundational rudiments. Weekly and yearly Premium subscriptions unlock all 40 rudiments, custom ladders, and full practice history. Current localized prices are shown by the App Store before purchase.
