Field Service: VisitCue

Updated October 5, 2026

A clear record of every visit

VisitCue helps independent service technicians plan visits, keep checklists, work notes, time, parts and photo evidence, and create reviewed service-report PDFs. This page provides support for VisitCue; it does not announce App Store availability.

Your first visit

Create a job, record what you actually did and review the report before sharing. Client records and visits can be edited or deleted. Deleting a client retains the visit snapshots. Visits are stored on your device; keep exported copies of important reports.

Optional AI

AI can organize selected written work notes with your permission. It needs an internet connection and an App Store or TestFlight installation for Apple authentication. A manual report stays available offline. AI does not inspect attached photos or certify equipment.

Plans

Free includes up to three active jobs, manual reports and PDF export, plus three successful AI drafts for the authenticated Apple app identity. Premium includes unlimited active jobs and reusable checklist access. Weekly includes 40 successful AI drafts per UTC ISO week; yearly includes 80. Actual prices appear in the app from Apple. No introductory trial is currently offered.

Help

If prices do not load, continue free and retry when connected. Restore an existing subscription from Settings. For missing data or a failed export, contact support before removing the app. Your local data is not stored in a VisitCue cloud account.
