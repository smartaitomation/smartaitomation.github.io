The pipeline problem

Random posting creates activity, not necessarily trust.

A feed full of isolated tips rarely shows how you think. Generic connection messages feel like a pitch before trust exists. Waiting for referrals keeps the pipeline outside your control.

The sprint gives the right people a reason to engage: a focused decision guide, published with context and shared manually with people for whom it is genuinely relevant.

The unique mechanism

The Proof Guide Loop.

Create one decision asset, distribute it selectively, and let real buyer questions sharpen both the guide and the paid offer.

  1. DiagnoseChoose one costly decision your buyer already needs to make.
  2. DistillTurn experience and credible sources into a short guide with a point of view.
  3. DistributePublish once and share selectively with people for whom it is relevant.
  4. DiscussAsk a real question, listen to the response, and qualify fit without forcing a pitch.
  5. DeepenUse recurring questions to improve the guide and shape your offer.

FAQ

Straight answers before you join.

Do I need a large following?

No. The output is designed to be useful to a small, relevant network and to support selective sharing.

Will you teach automation?

No. This sprint teaches manual research, relevance, permission, and respectful follow-up—not scraping or message bots.

Does this guarantee a client?

No. You control the quality of the guide and the conversations you start; buyers control whether and when they purchase.

Can AI write the guide?

AI can help structure and edit. You remain responsible for accuracy, original insight, permissions, and a human final draft.

How long does it take?

Plan 20–40 minutes per day, plus optional time for design and follow-up.
